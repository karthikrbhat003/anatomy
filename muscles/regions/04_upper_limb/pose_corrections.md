# 🦾 Upper Limb: Pose Corrections & Movement Applications

This index maps the weight-bearing, pushing, and balancing exercises for the shoulders, arms, and wrists to their dedicated, centralized biomechanical guides.

---

## 🧘‍♂️ Movement & Alignment Guides

For step-by-step instructions, joint safety notes, and sensory mind-muscle cues, visit the individual guides:

### 1. **[Handstand Forearm & Fingertip Balance](../../../pose_corrections/calisthenics/handstands.md)**
*   *Biomechanical Target:* Recruits deep forearm flexors and thenar/hypothenar muscles.
*   *🛡️ Joint Safety:* Uses the camber hand technique to protect the carpometacarpal joints from hyperextension.

### 2. **[Parallel Bar Dips](../../../pose_corrections/calisthenics/dips.md)**
*   *Biomechanical Target:* Concentrates load on Triceps Brachii, lower pectorals, and traps.
*   *🛡️ Joint Safety:* Keeps shoulders depressed and elbows tucked back to prevent rotator cuff pinch.

### 3. **[Handstand Push-ups & Overhead Press](../../../pose_corrections/calisthenics/handstands.md)**
*   *Biomechanical Target:* Deltoid heads and Triceps Brachii force generation.
*   *🛡️ Joint Safety:* Triangulates hands and head (tripod setup) to prevent subacromial impingement.

### 4. **[Adho Mukha Svanasana & Catur Svanasana (अधोमुखश्वानासन - Downward Dog & Dolphin Pose)](../../../pose_corrections/yoga/adho_mukha_svanasana.md)**
*   *Biomechanical Target:* Recruits Infraspinatus, Teres Minor, and Serratus Anterior.
*   *🛡️ Joint Safety:* Wraps outer armpits forward to prevent acromion impingement.

### 5. **[Wrist Protection (Hasta Bandha)](../../../pose_corrections/calisthenics/push_ups.md)**
*   *Biomechanical Target:* Active dome-knuckle support using forearm flexors.
*   *🛡️ Joint Safety:* Prevents palm dumping, relieving pressure in the carpal tunnel.
