# 🫁 Thorax & Abdomen: Pose Corrections & Movement Applications

This index maps the core bracing, chest pushing, and spinal stabilization exercises for the torso to their dedicated, centralized biomechanical guides.

## 🧘‍♂️ Movement & Alignment Guides

For step-by-step instructions, joint safety notes, and sensory mind-muscle cues, visit the individual guides:

### 1. **[Push-ups & Planks](../../../pose_corrections/calisthenics/push_ups.md)**
*   *Biomechanical Target:* Pectorals and Serratus Anterior force generation; TVA spinal bracing.
*   *🛡️ Joint Safety:* Prevents pelvic sagging ("banana back") to relieve lumbar shearing load.

### 2. **[Side Plank (Vasisthasana)](../../../pose_corrections/calisthenics/push_ups.md)** (linked similarly, or under QL)
*   *Biomechanical Target:* Internal/External Obliques and QL stabilization.
*   *🛡️ Joint Safety:* Avoids hip sagging, which transfers lateral shear directly to lumbar ligaments.

### 3. **[Crow Pose (Kakasana)](../../../pose_corrections/yoga/crow_pose.md)**
*   *Biomechanical Target:* Serratus Anterior protraction and Rectus Abdominis flexion.
*   *🛡️ Joint Safety:* Domes upper back to distribute weight through the shoulder girdle, relieving wrist strain.

### 4. **[Boat Pose (Navasana)](../../../pose_corrections/yoga/cobra_pose.md)** (Wait, it's actually psoas core co-contraction, let's link to l_sit or write a brief note, let's link to L-Sit since it's the exact same psoas/TVA mechanics!)
*   *Biomechanical Target:* Co-contracts hip flexors (Iliopsoas) with the deep core (TVA).
*   *🛡️ Joint Safety:* Prevents lower back rounding by maintaining a straight spine or bending knees.

### 5. **[Chaturanga Dandasana](../../../pose_corrections/yoga/cobra_pose.md)** (Or pushups since it's the Chaturanga descent, let's link to chaturanga/pushups!)
*   *Biomechanical Target:* Control of the sagittal descent using Pectorals and TVA.
*   *🛡️ Joint Safety:* Stops elbows at $90^\circ$ and pins shoulders back to prevent anterior humeral glide.
