# 🦵 Lower Limb: Pose Corrections & Movement Applications

This index maps the knee tracking, single-leg balancing, and hamstring release exercises for the lower body to their dedicated, centralized biomechanical guides.

---

## 🧘‍♂️ Movement & Alignment Guides

For step-by-step instructions, joint safety notes, and sensory mind-muscle cues, visit the individual guides:

### 1. **[Squats & Pistol Squats (Knee Tracking)](../../../pose_corrections/gym/squats.md)**
*   *Biomechanical Target:* Recruits Quadriceps Femoris and Gluteus Maximus.
*   *🛡️ Joint Safety:* Prevents inward knee cave (valgus collapse) to protect the ACL/MCL, and avoids "butt wink" disc load.

### 2. **[Virabhadrasana III (वीरभद्रासन ३ - Warrior III)](../../../pose_corrections/yoga/anjaneyasana.md)**
*   *Biomechanical Target:* Standing leg Gluteus Medius/Minimus isometric hold.
*   *🛡️ Joint Safety:* Prevents hip hiking and twisting in the standing hip socket to protect the acetabular labrum.

### 3. **[Uttanasana (उत्तानासन - Standing Forward Fold)](../../../pose_corrections/yoga/uttanasana.md)**
*   *Biomechanical Target:* Stretches the hamstrings; recruits quadriceps (reciprocal inhibition).
*   *🛡️ Joint Safety:* Keeps knees slightly bent to prevent proximal hamstring strain (yoga butt) at the ischial tuberosity.

### 4. **[Vrikshasana (वृक्षासन - Tree Pose)](../../../pose_corrections/yoga/vrikshasana.md)**
*   *Biomechanical Target:* Standing Gluteus Medius recruitment.
*   *🛡️ Joint Safety:* Prevents lateral standing hip flare, protecting the IT Band and hip socket.

### 5. **[Utthita Trikonasana (उत्थित त्रिकोणासन - Extended Triangle Pose)](../../../pose_corrections/yoga/utthita_trikonasana.md)**
*   *Biomechanical Target:* Recruits Gluteus Medius, Obliques, and Quadriceps; stretches front hamstrings and adductors.
*   *🛡️ Joint Safety:* Prevents front knee hyperextension and lateral lumbar collapsing.
