# 🔗 Master Skeletomuscular Map: Bone Landmarks & Muscle Attachments

This directory acts as the structural bridge between the **Skeletal System** and the **Muscular System**. Use this quick-reference index to trace how muscles anchor to specific bony landmarks, their mechanical leverage, and how to execute movements safely to prevent joint strain.

---

## 💀 1. Axial Skeleton Map (Skull, Spine, & Thorax)

| Bone & Landmark | Attached Muscle | Type | Joint Movement / Biomechanical Function | 🛡️ Joint Safety & Strain Prevention Tip |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Temporal Bone**<br>*(Mastoid Process)* | **[Sternocleidomastoid (SCM)](../regions/01_head_neck_face/anatomy.md#4-lateral--posterior-cervical-muscles)** | Insertion | Bilateral cervical flexion; unilateral cervical rotation to opposite side | **Cervical Compression:** Keep chin parallel to the floor during chin tucks to engage deep flexors rather than SCM, preventing facet joint strain. |
| **Temporal Bone**<br>*(Mastoid Process)* | **[Splenius Capitis](../regions/01_head_neck_face/anatomy.md#4-lateral--posterior-cervical-muscles)** | Insertion | Extends and rotates head/cervical spine | **Suboccipital Crunch:** Do not let the head drop passively back in Camel Pose; contract the SCM eccentrically to protect occipital nerves. |
| **Temporal Bone**<br>*(Styloid Process)* | **[Stylohyoid](../regions/01_head_neck_face/anatomy.md#3-anterior-neck--hyoid-muscles)** | Origin | Elevates and retracts the hyoid bone during swallowing | **TMJ Safety:** Squeeze the tongue to the roof of the mouth during neck exercises to stabilize the hyoid-jaw complex. |
| **Occipital Bone**<br>*(Superior Nuchal Line)* | **[Trapezius (Upper)](../regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-layer-appendicular-back-muscles)** | Origin | Elevates scapula; extends head/neck when scapula is fixed | **Neck Pinch:** Avoid shrugging shoulders during Downward Dog; depress scapulae to relieve pressure on C1-C2 facets. |
| **Mandible**<br>*(Angle & Ramus)* | **[Masseter](../regions/01_head_neck_face/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-mastication-chewing)** | Insertion | Elevates mandible (closes jaw), protracts jaw | **Jaw Tension:** Keep teeth slightly parted and jaw relaxed during physical exertion to prevent temporomandibular joint (TMJ) strain. |
| **Hyoid Bone**<br>*(Body & Horns)* | **[Suprahyoids / Infrahyoids](../regions/01_head_neck_face/anatomy.md#3-anterior-neck--hyoid-muscles)** | Multiple | Elevate/depress hyoid bone and larynx during swallowing/speech | **Throat Protection:** In neck backbends, do not collapse the throat; keep a slight tuck to protect the hyoid ligaments. |
| **Cervical Vertebrae (C1-C7)**<br>*(Transverse Processes)* | **[Scalenes (Ant/Med/Post)](../regions/01_head_neck_face/anatomy.md#4-lateral--posterior-cervical-muscles)** | Origin | Laterally flex and rotate neck; elevate Ribs 1-2 | **Nerve Impingement:** Avoid accessory breathing (shrugging upper chest to inhale); breathe diaphragmatically to prevent scalene spasm and thoracic outlet syndrome. |
| **Cervical Vertebrae (C1-C4)**<br>*(Transverse Processes)* | **[Levator Scapulae](../regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-layer-appendicular-back-muscles)** | Origin | Elevates scapula, downwardly rotates scapula, laterally flexes neck | **Stiff Neck:** Pull the shoulders down and wide during overhead presses to prevent the levator scapulae from seizing under load. |
| **Thoracic Spine (C7-T12)**<br>*(Spinous Processes)* | **[Trapezius](../regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-layer-appendicular-back-muscles)** | Origin | Scapular elevation (upper), retraction (middle), depression (lower) | **Shoulder Impingement:** Depress and retract shoulder blades in pull-ups to clear space in the subacromial joint. |
| **Thoracic/Lumbar Spine (T7-L5)**<br>*(Spinous Processes)* | **[Latissimus Dorsi](../regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-layer-appendicular-back-muscles)** | Origin | Extends, adducts, and internally rotates humerus | **Lumbar Hyperextension:** Squeeze glutes and abdominals during pull-ups to prevent the Latissimus from pulling the lower back into a painful arch. |
| **Lumbar Vertebrae (L1-L5)**<br>*(Transverse Processes / Bodies)* | **[Psoas Major](../regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#4-posterior-abdominal-wall)** | Origin | Flexes hip joint; flexes trunk when femur is fixed | **Lumbar Shearing:** Engage the deep lower core (TVA) in Boat Pose (Navasana) to brace the spine against the strong pull of the psoas. |
| **Lumbar Vertebrae (L1-L4)**<br>*(Transverse Processes)* | **[Quadratus Lumborum (QL)](../regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#4-posterior-abdominal-wall)** | Insertion | Laterally flexes lumbar spine (unilateral); extends spine (bilateral) | **SI Joint Shear:** Engage the obliques on the underside of a Side Plank to prevent the pelvis from sagging, saving the QL from straining. |
| **Sternum & Ribs 1-6**<br>*(Anterior Chest Wall)* | **[Pectoralis Major](../regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)** | Origin | Adducts, flexes, and internally rotates humerus | **Anterior Glide:** Keep shoulders pinned back at the bottom of push-ups/dips to prevent the head of the humerus from gliding forward and pinching the joint. |
| **Ribs 3-5**<br>*(Anterior Surface)* | **[Pectoralis Minor](../regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)** | Origin | Depresses and protracts the scapula | **Hunched Posture:** Stretch the pectoralis minor to release forward-rolled shoulders, which causes thoracic outlets. |
| **Ribs 1-8**<br>*(Outer Lateral Surfaces)* | **[Serratus Anterior](../regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)** | Origin | Protracts scapula and holds it flat against the ribs | **Winging Scapula:** At the top of planks/pushups, actively "push the ground away" to engage the serratus, stabilizing the shoulder girdle. |
| **Ribs 7-12 & L1-L3 bodies**<br>*(Costal Margin)* | **[Diaphragm](../regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-respiration--intercostals)** | Origin | Primary driver of respiration; contracts downward to draw air in | **Core Pressure:** Coordinate diaphragmatic breathing with pelvic floor contraction to manage intra-abdominal pressure safely. |

## 🦾 2. Appendicular Skeleton Map (Girdles & Limbs)

| Bone & Landmark | Attached Muscle | Type | Joint Movement / Biomechanical Function | 🛡️ Joint Safety & Strain Prevention Tip |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scapula**<br>*(Coracoid Process)* | **[Biceps Brachii (Short Head)](../regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-arm-brachium)** | Origin | Flexes elbow; supinates forearm; assists in shoulder flexion | **Biceps Tendonitis:** Do not flare elbows out in pushups/chaturanga; tuck them to $45^\circ$ to prevent tendon pinching at the coracoid. |
| **Scapula**<br>*(Coracoid Process)* | **[Pectoralis Minor](../regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)** | Insertion | Depresses and protracts the scapula | **Shoulder Impingement:** Avoid letting the shoulder blades wing; pull the shoulders down and wide to deactivate the pec minor. |
| **Scapula**<br>*(Supraspinous Fossa)* | **[Supraspinatus (Cuff)](../regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)** | Origin | Initiates humerus abduction (first 15°); stabilizes shoulder joint | **Impingement Zone:** Keep humerus externally rotated (wrap armpits forward) in Down Dog to prevent the acromion from pinching the tendon. |
| **Scapula**<br>*(Infraspinous Fossa)* | **[Infraspinatus (Cuff)](../regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)** | Origin | Externally rotates humerus; stabilizes shoulder joint | **Rotator Cuff Strain:** In handstand pushups, tuck elbows forward to recruit the infraspinatus for active glenohumeral stability. |
| **Scapula**<br>*(Lateral Border)* | **[Teres Minor (Cuff)](../regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)** | Origin | Externally rotates humerus; stabilizes shoulder joint | **Elbow Flaring:** Keep elbows tucked in parallel bar dips to avoid throwing the teres minor and rotator cuff into unstable strain. |
| **Scapula**<br>*(Subscapular Fossa)* | **[Subscapularis (Cuff)](../regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)** | Origin | Internally rotates humerus; stabilizes shoulder joint | **Shoulder Dislocation:** Squeeze the armpits in handstands to co-contract subscapularis, keeping the humeral head centered in the socket. |
| **Humerus**<br>*(Greater Tubercle)* | **[Rotator Cuff (SIT)](../regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)** | Insertion | Insertion point for Supraspinatus, Infraspinatus, and Teres Minor | **Joint Capsular Wear:** Avoid heavy pushing with internally rotated arms, which pulls the greater tubercle out of safe alignment. |
| **Humerus**<br>*(Lesser Tubercle)* | **[Subscapularis](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)** | Insertion | Internally rotates the humerus | **Tendon Subluxation:** Keep the bicipital groove aligned by avoiding extreme arm twisting under heavy loads. |
| **Humerus**<br>*(Deltoid Tuberosity)* | **[Deltoid (Ant/Mid/Post)](../regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)** | Insertion | Primary shoulder abductor (middle); flexor (anterior); extensor (posterior) | **Deltoid Strain:** Warm up the rotator cuff first; the deltoid is strong and will override the cuff, pulling the shoulder out of joint. |
| **Humerus**<br>*(Medial Epicondyle)* | **[Common Forearm Flexors](../regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#3-muscles-of-the-forearm-antebrachium)** | Origin | Common origin for wrist/finger flexor group | **Golfer's Elbow:** Avoid palm dumping (flat, passive hands) in plank; claw the mat (Hasta Bandha) to distribute load and prevent epicondylitis. |
| **Humerus**<br>*(Lateral Epicondyle)* | **[Common Forearm Extensors](../regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#3-muscles-of-the-forearm-antebrachium)** | Origin | Common origin for wrist/finger extensor group | **Tennis Elbow:** Keep wrist in line with forearm during pullups; do not let it collapse, which strains the lateral epicondyle insertion. |
| **Radius**<br>*(Radial Tuberosity)* | **[Biceps Brachii](../regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-arm-brachium)** | Insertion | Supinates forearm and flexes elbow joint | **Elbow Hyperextension:** In handstands, do not lock out and block the elbow joint; keep a micro-bend and engage the biceps to protect the ligaments. |
| **Ulna**<br>*(Olecranon Process)* | **[Triceps Brachii](../regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-arm-brachium)** | Insertion | Primary elbow joint extensor | **Triceps Tendonitis:** In overhead presses, avoid locking out with a sudden snap, which shocks the olecranon tendon insertion. |
| **Ilium**<br>*(ASIS)* | **[Sartorius](../regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-thigh)** | Origin | Flexes, abducts, and externally rotates the hip | **Knee Shearing:** Squeeze the thigh muscles during deep lunges to stabilize the Sartorius tendon tracking at the knee. |
| **Ilium**<br>*(AIIS)* | **[Rectus Femoris](../regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-thigh)** | Origin | Flexes hip; extends knee (spans two joints) | **Hip Impingement:** Keep the tailbone tucked (posterior tilt) in Crescent Lunge to stretch the rectus femoris without pinching the hip joint. |
| **Ischium**<br>*(Ischial Tuberosity)* | **[Hamstrings (Biceps/Semi)](../regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-thigh)** | Origin | Extends hip joint; flexes knee joint | **Yoga Butt (Tendonitis):** In Forward Fold, do not lock the knees; bend them slightly to release the direct pull on the tuberosity insertion. |
| **Femur**<br>*(Greater Trochanter)* | **[Gluteus Medius / Minimus](../regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region--deep-hip-rotators)** | Insertion | Primary hip abductors; stabilize pelvis during single-leg balance | **Pelvic Drop:** Contract the standing gluteus medius in Tree Pose to draw the outer hip inward, avoiding lateral hip joint capsule strain. |
| **Femur**<br>*(Lesser Trochanter)* | **[Iliopsoas (Psoas/Iliacus)](../regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#4-posterior-abdominal-wall)** | Insertion | Primary hip flexor linkage from spine/pelvis | **Hip Clicking:** Squeeze the glutes (antagonists) at the top of leg lifts to stretch the psoas tendon, preventing snapping hip syndrome. |
| **Tibia**<br>*(Tibial Tuberosity)* | **[Quadriceps Femoris](../regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-thigh)** | Insertion | Primary knee joint extensor group | **Patellar Shear:** Keep knees tracking straight over toes in squats; valgus collapse (inward cave) twists the patellar tendon, straining the tuberosity. |
| **Tibia**<br>*(Medial Condyle)* | **[Pes Anserinus Group](../regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-thigh)** | Insertion | Stabilizes the medial knee joint (Sartorius, Gracilis, Semitendinosus) | **MCL Strain:** Squeeze an imaginary block between the knees during bridge pose to recruit these stabilizers, protecting the medial knee. |
| **Calcaneus**<br>*(Heel Bone)* | **[Gastrocnemius / Soleus](../regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#3-muscles-of-the-leg-crus)** | Insertion | Primary plantarflexors of ankle (Achilles tendon) | **Achilles Strain:** Never bounce at the bottom of calf raises or jumps; control the eccentric phase to avoid tendon tears. |
