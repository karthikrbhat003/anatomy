# 🤸‍♂️ Front Lever (Calisthenics)

*   **Classification:** Straight-arm horizontal pulling hold; sagittal plane; Closed/Open Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Latissimus Dorsi](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-extrinsic-back-muscles)**, **[Teres Major](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)**, **[Pectoralis Major (Sternal Head)](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)**.
    *   *Synergists:* **[Triceps Brachii (Long Head)](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-arm-brachium)**, Posterior Deltoid, Rhomboids, Lower Trapezius.
    *   *Stabilizers:* **[Rectus Abdominis](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#3-abdominal-wall)**, **[Transverse Abdominis](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#3-abdominal-wall)**, Gluteus Maximus, Hamstrings.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Latissimus dorsi inserting into the intertubercular sulcus (bicipital groove) of the humerus; long head of triceps originating from the infraglenoid tubercle of the scapula.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  **Grip Setup:** Hang from the pull-up bar with an overhand (pronated) grip, slightly wider than shoulder-width. Wrap thumbs fully around the bar.
2.  **Scapular Lock:** Pull your shoulder blades down and back into **Active Scapular Depression and Retraction/Neutrality**.
3.  **Straight-Arm Pull-Down:** Drive your locked straight arms downward against the bar as if performing a straight-arm cable pulldown, engaging the lats and long head of the triceps.
4.  **Core & Hip Extension:** Brace the abdominal wall to resist lumbar hyperextension (anti-extension). Squeeze the glutes and adductors to raise the entire body parallel to the floor in a rigid horizontal plank.
5.  **Head & Neck:** Maintain a neutral cervical spine looking slightly upward at the bar.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Scapular Elevation / Shrug (Neck Collapse):** Allowing the scapulae to elevate into the ears places massive shear on the **rotator cuff** and **cervical spine**. Drive the lats down hard to keep the neck long.
*   **Lumbar Hyperextension (The "Pike & Sag" Fault):** If the abdominal wall fatigues, the pelvis drops and the lumbar spine sags into severe lordosis, creating excessive compressive shear on the **$L_5\text{–}S_1$ facet joints**. Regress to a tuck or straddle lever until core anti-extension strength matches lat pulling force.
*   **Distal Triceps Strain:** The long head of the triceps spans two joints (shoulder and elbow) and experiences peak isometric tensile strain at the elbow. Keep elbows completely straight rather than micro-bent to avoid joint torque imbalance.

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine snapping the pull-up bar in half and pulling it down to your hips.
*   *Resist:* Squeeze your thighs together like a single steel rod and lock your glutes to form a continuous rigid lever arm.
