# 🤸‍♂️ Planche & Planche Lean (Calisthenics)

*   **Classification:** Straight-arm horizontal pushing hold; sagittal plane; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Deltoid (Anterior)](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)**, **[Serratus Anterior](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)** (maximal protraction), **[Pectoralis Major (Clavicular Head)](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)**.
    *   *Synergists:* **[Biceps Brachii](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-arm-brachium)** (long head distal tendon isometric tension), Coracobrachialis, Lower Trapezius (depression).
    *   *Stabilizers:* **[Transverse Abdominis](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#3-abdominal-wall)**, **[Gluteus Maximus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)**, Quadriceps, Forearm Flexors.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Distal biceps tendon crossing the cubital fossa into the radial tuberosity; Serratus anterior inserting along the entire costal surface of the medial scapular border.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  **Hand Placement:** Place hands on floor or parallettes shoulder-width apart, fingers turned slightly outward ($30^\circ\text{–}45^\circ$) to reduce wrist extension shear.
2.  **Scapular Lock:** Maximize **Scapular Protraction** (pushing the floor away to round the upper back) and **Scapular Depression** (pulling shoulders away from the ears).
3.  **Elbow Lock:** Lock elbows into $100\%$ extension with the biceps facing forward (cubital fossa pointed forward).
4.  **Forward Lean:** Shift your center of mass forward over the wrists until your shoulders are significantly ahead of your hands.
5.  **Pelvic Alignment:** Engage glutes and lower abs to maintain a solid **Posterior Pelvic Tilt (PPT)** and hollow body shape. Lift toes off the floor into tuck, straddle, or full planche.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Distal Biceps Tendinopathy:** In straight-arm planche loading, the entire forward leverage moment arm is borne isometrically by the **Biceps Brachii tendon and anterior elbow joint capsule**. Never attempt high-lean volume with bent elbows or without months of progressive straight-arm conditioning (e.g., Planche Leans, Scapular Shrugs).
*   **Scapular Winging / Retraction Collapse:** If the Serratus Anterior fatigues and scapulae collapse into retraction, the humerus drives superiorly and anteriorly, causing severe **anterior shoulder capsule strain**. Terminate the set the moment protraction is lost.
*   **Wrist Compression:** Turn hands slightly outward or use parallettes to alleviate direct dorsal carpal impingement.

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine pushing the floor through the center of the earth while creating a protective dome with your upper back.
*   *Resist:* Grip the floor hard with your fingertips (Camber technique) and screw your elbow pits forward to lock the arm bones in pure compression.
