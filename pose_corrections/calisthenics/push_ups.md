# 🤸‍♂️ Push-ups & Planks (Calisthenics)

*   **Classification:** Sagittal/transverse plane push; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Pectoralis Major](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)**, **[Triceps Brachii](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-arm-brachium)**.
    *   *Synergists:* Anterior Deltoid, Serratus Anterior.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Transversus Abdominis (TVA), Gluteus Maximus.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Pectoralis Major inserts into the lateral lip of the intertubercular groove of the humerus.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Start in a high plank with hands directly under your shoulders, body in a straight line.
2.  Inhale and bend your elbows, lowering your chest to the floor. Keep your elbows tucked at a $45^\circ$ angle.
3.  Exhale and press back up. At the very top, push the floor away to dome your upper back slightly (scapular protraction).
4.  Keep your glutes squeezed and your tailbone tucked slightly (posterior pelvic tilt).

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Lumbar Shearing (Banana Back):** Letting your hips sag toward the floor compresses the **lumbar spine** under shearing load. Squeeze your glutes and pull your belly button to your spine to engage the TVA.
*   **Shoulder Flaring (90-Degree Flare):** Lowering with your elbows flaring out sideways at a $90^\circ$ angle pinches the **supraspinatus tendon** under the acromion. Keep elbows tucked to $45^\circ$ to keep the shoulder joint aligned.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine trying to "screw" your hands into the floor (right hand clockwise, left hand counter-clockwise) to lock your shoulders.
*   *Touch:* Feel the lower abdominal wall brace hard like a shield before descending.
