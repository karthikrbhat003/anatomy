# 🤸‍♂️ Pistol Squat / Single-Leg Squat (Calisthenics)

*   **Classification:** Unilateral lower-body squat / balance; sagittal plane; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists (Working Leg):* **[Quadriceps Femoris (Rectus Femoris, Vasti)](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#3-anterior-thigh-quadriceps)**, **[Gluteus Maximus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)**.
    *   *Agonists (Elevated Leg):* **[Iliopsoas](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-anterior-hip-flexors)**, **[Rectus Femoris](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#3-anterior-thigh-quadriceps)** (isometric hip flexion).
    *   *Stabilizers:* **[Gluteus Medius/Minimus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)** (pelvic level balance), **[Tibialis Anterior](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#5-anterior-leg)** (dorsiflexion), Core.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Tibial tuberosity (patellar tendon insertion); greater trochanter of the femur (gluteal insertion).

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  **Stance & Foot Grip:** Stand on one leg. Spread your toes and grip the floor with the **Tripod Foot** (calcaneus, $1^{\text{st}}$ metatarsal head, $5^{\text{th}}$ metatarsal head).
2.  **Free Leg Extension:** Extend the opposite leg straight forward in front of you, firing the quads and hip flexors to keep the heel elevated off the floor.
3.  **Descent (Hip Hinge & Knee Flexion):** Reach your arms forward for counterbalance. Hinge at the hip and bend the working knee, tracking the knee directly in line with the second toe.
4.  **Bottom Position:** Descend until the working hamstring touches the calf while maintaining an active foot arch (heel remains flat on the floor).
5.  **Drive to Lockout:** Push firmly through the midfoot and heel, firing the glutes and quadriceps to ascend without hip rotation.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Knee Valgus Collapse (Inward Tracking):** Allowing the knee to collapse inward during ascent strains the **Medial Collateral Ligament (MCL)** and creates patellofemoral shear. Engage the **Gluteus Medius** to maintain dynamic external torque.
*   **Heel Rising / Dorsiflexion Restriction:** If ankle dorsiflexion is restricted ($<35^\circ\text{–}40^\circ$), the heel pops up, placing extreme compressive stress on the **patellar tendon and retropatellar cartilage**. Elevate the heel slightly on a small plate while working on calf mobility.
*   **Spinal Butt-Wink / Severe Lumbar Flexion:** While some thoracic rounding is natural for counterbalance, excessive lumbar flexion under load stresses the **posterior annulus fibrosus** of lumbar discs.

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine screwing your foot into the floor like a corkscrew to create an unshakeable base of support.
*   *Resist:* Reach your arms forward and chest up as if reaching for an object just out of reach to balance your center of mass.
