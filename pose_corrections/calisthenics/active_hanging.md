# 🤸‍♂️ Active Hanging & Scapular Pull-ups (Calisthenics)

*   **Classification:** Isometric / concentric hold; sagittal plane; Closed Kinetic Chain (hands fixed, body moves).
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Lower Trapezius](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-layer-appendicular-back-muscles)**, **[Middle Trapezius](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-layer-appendicular-back-muscles)**, **[Rhomboid Major/Minor](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-layer-appendicular-back-muscles)**.
    *   *Antagonists (stretched):* Pectoralis Major/Minor, Latissimus Dorsi.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Lower/middle trapezius insert into the spine of the scapula and acromion; rhomboids insert into the medial border of the scapula.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Hang from a overhead bar with a shoulder-width overhand grip, arms completely straight.
2.  Without bending your elbows, pull your shoulder blades down and together, drawing your head and chest upward away from the floor.
3.  Hold this contracted position for 2–3 seconds, maintaining a tight core and neutral neck.
4.  Slowly lower back down under control to a passive dead hang.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Rotator Cuff and Labral Shearing:** Allowing your shoulders to shrug up to touch your ears under passive body weight load (dead hang without muscular support) stretches and destabilizes the **shoulder joint capsule and labrum**, which can lead to labral tears or shoulder impingement. Always "pack" the shoulders by pulling the scapulae down to protect the glenohumeral joint.
*   **Neck Strain:** Do not crane your neck or try to look at the bar; keep your chin tucked slightly to prevent straining the levator scapulae and cervical vertebrae.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine trying to slide your shoulder blades down your back into your back pockets.
*   *Resist:* As you lower, resist gravity slowly, keeping the shoulder girdle active until the very end.
