# 🤸‍♂️ Handstand Balancing & Push-ups (Calisthenics)

*   **Classification:** Vertical overhead pushing / isometric balance; frontal/sagittal plane; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Deltoid (Anterior/Lateral)](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)**, **[Triceps Brachii](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-arm-brachium)**.
    *   *Synergists:* Serratus Anterior, Upper Trapezius, Forearm Flexors.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Rotator Cuff (Infraspinatus/Subscapularis), Core, Gluteus Maximus.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Forearm flexors attach to the medial epicondyle of the humerus; deltoids insert into the deltoid tuberosity of the humerus.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Place hands slightly wider than shoulder-width, fingers spread. Use the **Camber Hand Technique**: Cup your hands slightly so there is a small gap under the knuckles, and press your fingertips firmly into the floor.
2.  Kick up into a handstand, squeezing your legs and pointing your toes.
3.  For handstand pushups, lower your head *forward* of your hands to form a tripod, bending your elbows to $90^\circ$. Keep elbows tucked forward at a $45^\circ$ angle.
4.  Press back up, push the floor away, and return to vertical alignment.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Acromion Tendon Pinching (T-Shape Flaring):** Flaring your elbows out to the sides in a straight line with the shoulders (forming a T-shape) forces the humerus into internal rotation under load, causing **subacromial shoulder impingement** by pinching the **supraspinatus tendon** against the acromion. Keep elbows tucked at a $45^\circ$ angle.
*   **Wrist Hyperextension:** Flat, passive hands dump all weight into the heel of the palm, overloading the **carpometacarpal joints**. Dig your fingertips actively into the floor (camber hand technique) to distribute weight.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine pushing the floor away from your body to grow taller.
*   *Resist:* As you balance, think of your fingertips as active piano players, tapping and pressing to maintain control.
