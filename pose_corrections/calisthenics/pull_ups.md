# 🤸‍♂️ Pull-ups & Lat Pulldowns (Calisthenics)

*   **Classification:** Sagittal/Frontal plane pulling movement; Closed Kinetic Chain (Pull-up) or Open Kinetic Chain (Lat Pulldown).
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonist:* **[Latissimus Dorsi](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-layer-appendicular-back-muscles)**.
    *   *Synergists:* Teres Major, Rhomboids, Lower/Middle Trapezius, Biceps Brachii, Brachialis.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Core (TVA, Rectus Abdominis), Gluteus Maximus.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Latissimus Dorsi originates at T7-L5 spinous processes, the thoracolumbar fascia, and the iliac crest; inserts into the intertubercular groove of the humerus.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width, palms facing away.
2.  Start from a dead hang, then actively pack your shoulders down (scapular pull-up).
3.  Pull your chest up toward the bar by driving your elbows down into your imaginary side pockets.
4.  Maintain a tight hollow-body position (squeeze glutes, legs straight and slightly in front of you).
5.  Clear the bar with your chin, then lower under control.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Anterior Humeral Glide (Elbow Flaring):** Pulling with your elbows flaring backward forces the humeral head to glide forward, stressing the **anterior shoulder capsule** and pinching the **supraspinatus tendon**. Keep your chest tall and elbows pointing slightly forward.
*   **Lumbar Hyperextension:** Squeeze your glutes and pull your belly button to your spine to prevent the Latissimus from pulling your lower back into an excessive, painful arch under load.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine pulling the bar down to your chest rather than pulling your body up.
*   *Resist:* On the descent, resist gravity slowly, imagining you are pushing the bar away from you.
