# 🤸‍♂️ L-Sit Hold (Calisthenics)

*   **Classification:** Isometric hold; sagittal plane; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Iliopsoas](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#4-posterior-abdominal-wall)**, **[Rectus Femoris](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-thigh)**.
    *   *Synergists:* Rectus Abdominis, Obliques, Pectineus, Sartorius.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Triceps Brachii, Lower Trapezius, Serratus Anterior.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Iliopsoas inserts into the lesser trochanter of the femur; Lower Trapezius inserts into the spine of the scapula.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Sit on the floor or support bars, placing your hands next to your thighs.
2.  Press down firmly through your hands, depressing your shoulder blades to lift your hips off the ground. Keep your chest tall.
3.  Lock your knees out completely and lift your legs parallel to the floor, forming an "L" shape.
4.  Hold the position, breathing shallowly through the nose.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Neck and Shoulder Compression:** Sinking into the shoulders under load stresses the neck and cervical spine. Actively push the floor/bars away (Serratus Anterior and Lower Trapezius engagement) to elevate the torso.
*   **Hip Flexor Cramping:** If the hip flexors cramp, drop to a tucked L-sit (knees bent); this shortens the lever arm, relieving strain on the iliopsoas tendon insertion.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine pulling your thigh bones deep into your hip sockets to lift them higher.
*   *Touch:* Feel the lower abdominal wall brace hard like a corset.
