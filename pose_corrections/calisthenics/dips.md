# 🤸‍♂️ Parallel Bar Dips (Calisthenics)

*   **Classification:** Sagittal/Frontal plane pushing; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Triceps Brachii](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-arm-brachium)**, **[Pectoralis Major (Lower/Sternal)](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)**.
    *   *Synergists:* Anterior Deltoid, Pectoralis Minor.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Lower Trapezius, Serratus Anterior.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Triceps insert into the olecranon process of the ulna; Pectoralis Major inserts into the greater tubercle lateral lip of the humerus.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Support yourself on the bars with locked elbows, shoulders pushed down (away from ears).
2.  Lean your torso slightly forward ($10-15^\circ$) to recruit the chest.
3.  Inhale and bend your elbows, lowering your body until your shoulders are slightly below elbow height ($90^\circ$ elbow bend).
4.  Press up by extending your elbows, exhaling, and returning to the support position.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Shoulder Dumping (Internal Humerus Rotation):** Letting your elbows flare outward and shoulders roll forward at the bottom forces the **anterior shoulder joint capsule** and the rotator cuff to bear your body weight, causing impingement and labral tears. Keep your chest proud, shoulders depressed (lower traps active), and tuck your elbows straight back.
*   **Hyperextension Limit:** Stop descending when your elbows reach a $90^\circ$ angle; going deeper increases shearing force on the shoulder joint capsule.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine trying to squeeze the two parallel bars closer together as you press upward.
*   *Resist:* As you lower, squeeze your armpits tight to keep the stabilizers active.
