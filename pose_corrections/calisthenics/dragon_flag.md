# 🤸‍♂️ Dragon Flag (Calisthenics)

*   **Classification:** Straight-body anterior core anti-extension lever; sagittal plane; Closed/Open Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Rectus Abdominis](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#3-abdominal-wall)**, **[Transverse Abdominis](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#3-abdominal-wall)**, **[External/Internal Obliques](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#3-abdominal-wall)**.
    *   *Synergists:* **[Latissimus Dorsi](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-extrinsic-back-muscles)**, **[Gluteus Maximus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)**, Hamstrings, Quadriceps.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Posterior Deltoids, Rhomboids, Triceps Brachii.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Pubic crest and symphysis (rectus abdominis origin); xiphoid process and costal cartilages $5\text{–}7$ (insertion).

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  **Bench Anchor Setup:** Lie supine on a sturdy bench or floor. Reach overhead and grip the edges of the bench or a secure upright pole tightly behind your head.
2.  **Candlestick Lift:** Drive your elbows into the bench, engage your lats, and roll your hips up off the bench until your entire body is balanced vertically on your upper shoulder blades and upper back (cervical spine remains unloaded).
3.  **Hollow Body Lock:** Squeeze your glutes, lock your knees, point your toes, and tuck your pelvis into a **Posterior Pelvic Tilt (PPT)**. The body forms a rigid, unbroken straight line.
4.  **Controlled Eccentric Lowering:** Lower your entire torso and legs together as a single rigid unit toward the bench, pivoting strictly from the upper back.
5.  **Reversal:** Stop just 1–2 inches above the bench without allowing hips or lower back to touch, then pull back up to vertical using pure abdominal and lat pulling strength.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Cervical Spine Compression:** Never rest your weight on your neck or head. The pivot point must remain strictly across the **upper thoracic spine and scapular shelf**.
*   **Lumbar Hyperextension (Belly Flaring):** If the abdominal wall gives out, the lumbar spine arches into severe hyperextension, transferring the entire load onto the **anterior longitudinal ligament and lumbar facet joints**. Stop the movement or bend the knees into a tuck dragon flag the moment PPT is lost.
*   **Bending at the Hips (Piking):** Piking at the hips turns the exercise into a hip flexor drill rather than an anti-extension core movement. Keep the hip angle locked at $180^\circ$.

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine your body is an unbending plank of solid oak anchored to the floor at the shoulders.
*   *Resist:* Pull your ribs down toward your pelvis to maintain a continuous abdominal vacuum brace throughout the entire descent.
