# 🤸‍♂️ Back Lever (Calisthenics)

*   **Classification:** Straight-arm shoulder hyperextension hold; sagittal plane; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Biceps Brachii](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-arm-brachium)** (long/short heads under end-range tensile stretch), **[Pectoralis Major](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)**, **[Anterior Deltoid](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)**.
    *   *Synergists:* **[Latissimus Dorsi](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-extrinsic-back-muscles)**, Brachialis, Coracobrachialis.
    *   *Stabilizers:* **[Erector Spinae](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#2-deep-intrinsic-back-muscles)**, **[Gluteus Maximus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)**, Hamstrings, Scapular Depressors/Protractors.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Coracoid process of the scapula (origin of short head of biceps and coracobrachialis); radial tuberosity (biceps insertion).

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  **Grip Selection:**
    *   *Supinated Grip (German Hang style - Palm up/facing away):* Higher biceps tendon recruitment and mobility demand.
    *   *Pronated Grip (Palms facing down):* Lower risk for beginners, engages latissimus dorsi and posterior deltoids more heavily.
2.  **Skin the Cat Entry:** Invert into an upside-down hang and slowly lower hips and legs backward through the arms into a **German Hang**.
3.  **Scapular Protraction & Depression:** Pull the shoulder blades down and spread them wide across the rib cage.
4.  **Straight-Body Extension:** Squeeze glutes, hamstrings, and lower back to bring the torso and legs parallel to the floor, facing downward.
5.  **Elbow Lock:** Lock elbows to $100\%$ extension with biceps tension.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Distal Biceps Tendon Rupture Risk:** The supinated back lever places the **distal biceps tendon** under the highest tensile and shear load of any gymnastic skill. Never jump into a full supinated back lever without progressive connective tissue adaptation (months of German hangs, skin-the-cats, and tuck lever progressions).
*   **Anterior Capsule Overstretching:** Without active scapular protraction and pectoralis bracing, the humeral head is forced anteriorly, stretching the **anterior glenohumeral ligament**.
*   **Excessive Spinal Arching:** Maintain a slight hollow or neutral body line to avoid hyper-extending the thoracolumbar junction.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine pulling the bar into your lower back while puffing your upper back up to the ceiling.
*   *Resist:* Flex your triceps and biceps simultaneously (co-contraction) to create a protective muscular sleeve around your elbow joints.
