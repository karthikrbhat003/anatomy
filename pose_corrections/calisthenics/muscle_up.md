# 🤸‍♂️ Bar & Ring Muscle-Up (Calisthenics)

*   **Classification:** Multi-planar dynamic pulling-to-pushing compound transition; sagittal plane; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Pulling Phase:* **[Latissimus Dorsi](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-extrinsic-back-muscles)**, **[Biceps Brachii](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-arm-brachium)**, Brachialis, Posterior Deltoid, Rhomboids.
    *   *Transition Phase:* **[Rotator Cuff (Subscapularis/Infraspinatus)](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)**, Pectoralis Minor, Forearm Flexors (*False Grip*).
    *   *Pushing / Dip Phase:* **[Triceps Brachii](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-arm-brachium)**, **[Pectoralis Major](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)**, Anterior Deltoid.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Medial epicondyle of humerus (flexor origin); sternal and clavicular attachments of pectoralis major.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  **Grip Setup:** On rings, use a **False Grip** (resting the base of the wrist / pisiform bone over the wood of the ring). On a straight bar, use a deep overhand grip with wrists slightly rotated over the bar.
2.  **Explosive High Pull:** Pull explosively downward and backward, driving your chest toward the bar (pulling to the sternum or upper abdomen, not just the chin).
3.  **The Fast Transition:** At the peak of the pull, rapidly rotate your chest and shoulders *over* the bar/rings by driving your elbows backward and up, bringing the torso into an aggressive forward lean.
4.  **Straight-Bar / Ring Dip Lockout:** Press firmly through the triceps and chest to lock out the arms at the top of the dip.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **The "Chicken Wing" Single-Arm Flaw:** Forcing one arm over the bar first while the other lags behind places the leading shoulder into extreme uncontrolled internal rotation and anterior humeral glide under bodyweight load. This is a primary mechanism for **glenoid labral tears (SLAP lesions)** and **rotator cuff tendonitis**. If you cannot clear both elbows simultaneously, develop higher pulling power first.
*   **Anterior Humeral Glide at Transition:** Keep the shoulders depressed and chest active as you transition over the bar to prevent the humeral head from sliding forward against the anterior glenohumeral capsule.
*   **Wrist Strain in False Grip:** Condition the wrist flexors gradually to avoid compressing the **median nerve** in the carpal tunnel.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Think of pulling the bar down to your belly button rather than pulling your chin up to the bar.
*   *Resist:* Head-butt through the invisible window at the top of the pull to transition shoulders smoothly over hands.
