# 🏋️ Barbell Bench Press (Gym)

*   **Classification:** Transverse plane horizontal push; Open Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Pectoralis Major](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)**, **[Triceps Brachii](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-arm-brachium)**.
    *   *Synergists:* Anterior Deltoid.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Rotator Cuff, Rhomboids, Latissimus Dorsi.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Pectoralis Major inserts into the lateral lip of the intertubercular groove of the humerus.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Lie flat on the bench, feet pressed firmly into the floor.
2.  Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width.
3.  Unrack the bar and hold it directly over your chest. Pin your shoulder blades down and back into the bench.
4.  Lower the bar under control to your mid-chest, keeping your elbows tucked at a $45-75^\circ$ angle.
5.  Press the bar back up in a slight diagonal arc, ending directly over your shoulders.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **90-Degree Elbow Flare:** Do not flare your elbows out at $90^\circ$ (aligned with shoulders). This pinches the **supraspinatus tendon** under the acromion process. Tuck elbows to $45-75^\circ$ to keep the shoulder joint aligned.
*   **Losing Scapular Setup:** Do not let your shoulders roll forward off the bench as you press. This causes the humeral head to glide forward, straining the **anterior shoulder capsule**. Keep your shoulder blades pinned back.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine trying to bend the barbell in half (ends pointing away from you) to lock in your back muscles.
*   *Resist:* As the bar touches your chest, think of your chest as a compressed spring ready to release.
