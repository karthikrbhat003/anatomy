# 🏋️ Dumbbell Lateral Raise (Gym / Weight Training)

*   **Classification:** Single-joint shoulder abduction; scapular plane ($30^\circ$ anterior to frontal plane); Open Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Deltoid (Lateral / Middle Head)](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)**.
    *   *Synergists:* **[Supraspinatus](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)** (initiates first $0^\circ\text{–}15^\circ$ of abduction), **[Trapezius (Upper/Lower)](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-extrinsic-back-muscles)**, **[Serratus Anterior](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)** (upward scapular rotation beyond $60^\circ$).
    *   *Stabilizers:* **[Rotator Cuff (Infraspinatus, Subscapularis)](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)**, Core, Forearm Extensors.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Acromion process of scapula; deltoid tuberosity of humerus; greater tubercle of humerus.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  **Stance & Posture:** Stand tall with feet shoulder-width apart, knees slightly unlocked, core braced, and torso with a slight forward lean ($10^\circ\text{–}15^\circ$).
2.  **The Scapular Plane (Scaption):** Position your arms **$30^\circ$ forward of the pure frontal plane** (in line with the anatomical angle of the scapular blade), rather than straight out to the sides.
3.  **Neutral Shoulder Rotation:** Grip dumbbells with palms facing each other (neutral) or angled slightly forward. **Do not internally rotate the shoulders** (avoid "pouring out a pitcher of water" / empty-can posture).
4.  **Raise with Elbows Leading:** Sweep the dumbbells out and away from your sides in a wide arc, leading with your elbows and keeping a fixed $15^\circ$ bend at the elbow.
5.  **Peak Height & Lowering:** Raise until the dumbbells reach shoulder height (parallel to floor, $90^\circ$ abduction). Lower smoothly with a 2-second eccentric phase.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **The "Empty Can" Internal Rotation Impingement:** Raising the arms while intentionally internally rotating the shoulders (thumbs pointing down) drives the **greater tubercle of the humerus** directly into the **coracoacromial arch**, severely pinching the **supraspinatus tendon and subacromial bursa**. Keep the thumbs level with or slightly higher than the pinkies.
*   **Pure Frontal Plane Movement:** Lifting straight out to the true lateral sides forces the humerus to scrape against the acromial edge. Always lift in the **Scapular Plane ($30^\circ$ anterior)**.
*   **Upper Trapezius Shrugging / Momentum:** Shrugging the neck to initiate the lift turns the exercise into a trapezius compensation and jams the cervical spine. Keep the scapulae depressed and move strictly from the lateral deltoid.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine reaching your knuckles outward to touch the side walls rather than lifting the weights up to the ceiling.
*   *Resist:* Pour your effort into leading with the lateral elbows, keeping your neck completely loose and relaxed.
