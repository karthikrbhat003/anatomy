# 🏋️ Conventional & Romanian Deadlifts (Gym)

*   **Classification:** Sagittal plane posterior chain hip hinge; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Gluteus Maximus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region--deep-hip-rotators)**, **[Hamstrings](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-thigh)** (Biceps Femoris, Semitendinosus, Semimembranosus).
    *   *Synergists:* Adductor Magnus, Quadriceps, Gastrocnemius.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Erector Spinae, Trapezius, Latissimus Dorsi, Core.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Hamstrings originate at the ischial tuberosity; Erectors stabilize the spinal column.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Stand with feet hip-width apart, shins about 1 inch away from the barbell.
2.  Hinge at your hips and bend your knees slightly to grip the bar. Keep your spine long.
3.  Pull your shoulder blades down (engaging the Latissimus Dorsi) to "take the slack out of the bar."
4.  Drive through your legs, pushing the floor away to lift the bar in a straight vertical path close to your body.
5.  Lock out by squeezing your glutes, keeping your spine straight. Do not lean back.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Spinal Rounding (Cat Back):** Lifting with a rounded lumbar spine places extreme compressive and shearing forces on the **lumbar discs**, risking herniation. Keep your spine flat.
*   **Lumbar Overextension:** Do not lean backward at the top. This pinches the **facet joints** of the lumbar spine. Stop the lockout when your shoulders are stacked over your hips.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine your legs are a hydraulic press pushing the earth away from you.
*   *Resist:* Pull your shoulder blades down into your "back pockets" to secure your upper spine.
