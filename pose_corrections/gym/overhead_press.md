# 🏋️ Barbell & Dumbbell Overhead Press (Gym)

*   **Classification:** Vertical overhead push; frontal/sagittal plane; Open Kinetic Chain (weight moves, body stable).
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Deltoid (Anterior/Lateral)](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)**, **[Triceps Brachii](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-arm-brachium)**.
    *   *Synergists:* Serratus Anterior, Upper Trapezius, Pectoralis Major (Clavicular).
    *   *Stabilizers:* Rotator Cuff, Core, Gluteus Maximus.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Deltoids insert into the deltoid tuberosity of the humerus; triceps insert into the olecranon process of the ulna.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Hold the barbell at collarbone height, hands slightly wider than shoulder-width, elbows tucked forward under the bar.
2.  Brace your core, squeeze your glutes, and tuck your chin back slightly.
3.  Press the bar straight up overhead, moving your head forward once the bar clears your face.
4.  Lock out overhead, shrug your shoulders up slightly to engage the traps and stabilize the joint.
5.  Lower under control to your chest.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Lumbar Overextension (Back Arching):** Arching your lower back to press the weight forward shifts the load into a chest-press position, but compresses the **lumbar facet joints** under heavy vertical load. Squeeze your glutes and brace your core to maintain a neutral spine.
*   **Elbow Flaring:** Do not let the elbows flare outward sideways during the press; this causes **subacromial shoulder impingement**. Keep elbows tucked at a $45^\circ$ angle forward.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine pushing yourself away from the bar into the floor.
*   *Touch:* Squeeze your glutes and core to create a solid base.
