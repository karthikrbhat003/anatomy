# 🏋️ Bulgarian Split Squat (Rear-Foot Elevated Split Squat) (Gym / Weight Training)

*   **Classification:** Unilateral lower-body squat / knee & hip flexion; sagittal plane; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Lead Leg Agonists:* **[Quadriceps Femoris](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#3-anterior-thigh-quadriceps)**, **[Gluteus Maximus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)**, **[Adductor Magnus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-medial-thigh-adductors)**.
    *   *Rear Leg (Eccentric Stretch):* **[Rectus Femoris](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#3-anterior-thigh-quadriceps)**, **[Iliopsoas](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-anterior-hip-flexors)**.
    *   *Stabilizers:* **[Gluteus Medius/Minimus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)** (prevents pelvic drop), Core, Foot Intrinsics.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Greater trochanter of femur; tibial tuberosity; anterior superior iliac spine (ASIS).

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  **Bench Setup & Stance:** Stand facing away from a sturdy bench ($12\text{–}16\text{ inches}$ high). Rest the top of your rear foot (shoelaces down) on the edge of the bench.
2.  **Lead Foot Placement:** Step your front foot forward far enough so that at the bottom of the squat, your front shin is vertical or slightly forward-angled, and your front knee forms a $\approx 90^\circ$ angle.
3.  **Torso Angle:** Maintain a slight forward torso lean ($15^\circ\text{–}20^\circ$) aligned with your rear hip to load the front **Gluteus Maximus** and minimize shear on the lumbar spine.
4.  **Descent:** Lower your hips down and slightly back until your rear knee hovers 1 inch above the floor.
5.  **Ascent:** Drive firmly through the whole front foot (especially the midfoot and heel), extending both hip and knee to return to the starting position.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Front Knee Valgus Collapse:** Allowing the lead knee to dive inward under heavy dumbbell loads places high tensile strain on the **MCL** and **anterior cruciate ligament (ACL)**. Actively push the front knee outward to track over the pinky toes.
*   **Hyperextending the Lumbar Spine:** Standing too upright while forcing the rear leg back pulls on the tight **Iliopsoas and Rectus Femoris**, tilting the pelvis anteriorly and pinching the **lumbar facet joints**. Maintain a slight forward hinge of the torso.
*   **Excessive Rear Leg Pushing:** Bouncing off the back foot unloads the front working leg. Treat the rear foot strictly as a kickstand for balance; **$85\text{–}90\%$ of the load must remain on the front leg**.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine driving your front heel straight down through a floor scale to move the needle as high as possible.
*   *Resist:* Squeeze your front glute at the bottom of the rep to propel your hips forward and up like a piston.
