# 🏋️ Barbell Back Squat (Gym)

*   **Classification:** Sagittal plane compound lower body push; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Quadriceps Femoris](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-thigh)** (Rectus Femoris, Vastus Lateralis/Medialis/Intermedius), **[Gluteus Maximus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region--deep-hip-rotators)**.
    *   *Synergists:* Adductor Magnus, Soleus.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Erector Spinae, Transversus Abdominis (TVA), Gluteus Medius.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Quadriceps insert into the tibial tuberosity via the patellar tendon; Gluteus Maximus inserts into the gluteal tuberosity of the femur and the IT Band.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Rest the barbell on your upper traps. Stand with feet slightly wider than hip-width, toes turned slightly out ($15-30^\circ$).
2.  Inhale and brace your core (Valsalva maneuver). Hinge your hips back and bend your knees.
3.  Lower your body, keeping your knees tracking in line with your feet.
4.  Go down until your hip joint crease is below the top of your knee joint ($90^\circ+$ depth).
5.  Drive up through the mid-foot, keeping your chest tall.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Knee Cave (Valgus Collapse):** Do not let your knees collapse inward. This places severe shearing forces on the **ACL** and compresses the **lateral meniscus**. Focus on pushing your knees outward (recruiting the Gluteus Medius).
*   **Butt Wink (Lumbar Flexion):** Avoid rounding your lower back at the bottom of the squat. This puts a high flexion load on the **lumbar intervertebral discs**. Squat only as deep as your neutral spine allows.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine trying to "spread the floor apart" laterally with your feet.
*   *Resist:* Drive up by pushing the floor away.
