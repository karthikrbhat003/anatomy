# 🏋️ Barbell Rows & Lat Pulldowns (Gym)

*   **Classification:** Sagittal/frontal plane pull; Open Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonist:* **[Latissimus Dorsi](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-layer-appendicular-back-muscles)**, **[Rhomboid Major/Minor](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-layer-appendicular-back-muscles)**.
    *   *Synergists:* Trapezius, Teres Major, Biceps Brachii, Brachialis.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Erector Spinae, Core.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Latissimus Dorsi inserts into the intertubercular groove of the humerus; rhomboids insert into the medial border of the scapula.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution (Barbell Row)
1.  Hold the barbell with a shoulder-width grip, hinge at your hips until your torso is at a $45-90^\circ$ angle to the floor. Keep your spine flat.
2.  Pull the bar toward your lower chest / upper abdomen, leading with your elbows.
3.  Squeeze your shoulder blades together at the top of the movement.
4.  Lower the bar under control, extending your arms fully without letting your spine round.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Torso Bouncing (Lumbar Shearing):** Avoid using momentum (bouncing your torso up and down) to lift the weight. This transfers heavy shearing force to the **lumbar spine**. Keep your torso stationary.
*   **Shoulder Internal Rotation:** Do not pull the bar too high (to the collarbones) with flared elbows. This internally rotates the humerus and pinches the **shoulder cuff tendons**. Pull toward your belly button, keeping elbows close to the body.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine your hands are just hooks, and you are pulling the weight solely with your elbows.
*   *Touch:* Squeeze the shoulder blades together at the top as if squeezing a pencil between them.
