# 🏋️ Barbell Hip Thrust (Gym / Weight Training)

*   **Classification:** Bilateral supine hip extension; sagittal plane; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Gluteus Maximus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)** (peak mechanical tension at full extension).
    *   *Synergists:* **[Hamstrings](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#4-posterior-thigh-hamstrings)**, **[Adductor Magnus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-medial-thigh-adductors)**, **[Erector Spinae](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#2-deep-intrinsic-back-muscles)**.
    *   *Stabilizers:* **[Quadriceps Femoris](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#3-anterior-thigh-quadriceps)**, **[Transverse Abdominis](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#3-abdominal-wall)**, Gluteus Medius (abduction torque).
*   **Key Landmarks:** Greater trochanter of femur; inferior angle of scapula; anterior superior iliac spine (ASIS).

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  **Bench Setup:** Sit on the floor with your upper back (just below the inferior scapular angle) resting firmly against the edge of a sturdy bench ($14\text{–}16\text{ inches}$ high).
2.  **Barbell Placement:** Roll a padded barbell directly over your hips, resting across the pelvic crease (ASIS).
3.  **Foot Stance:** Place feet shoulder-width apart, toes turned slightly outward ($15^\circ$). Position feet so that at the top of the thrust, your **shins are strictly vertical ($90^\circ$ knee angle)**.
4.  **Chin Tuck & Eye Gaze:** Keep your chin tucked toward your chest and look straight forward across the room at all times (do not look up at the ceiling).
5.  **Drive to Lockout:** Drive through your heels, thrusting your hips upward into **Full Hip Extension** and locking into a **Posterior Pelvic Tilt (PPT)** at the top. Squeeze your glutes for a full 1-second isometric peak contraction.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Lumbar Spinal Hyperextension:** Looking up at the ceiling or overarching the lower back to get the barbell higher transfers the load from the glutes to the **lumbar intervertebral discs and facet joints**. Lock the rib cage down and achieve lockout via **pure pelvic posterior tilt and hip extension**.
*   **Shins Slanting Forward/Backward:**
    *   *Feet too far forward:* Shins angle backward, over-recruiting the hamstrings and under-recruiting the glutes.
    *   *Feet too close to bench:* Shins angle forward, over-recruiting the quads and creating anterior patellofemoral shear. Ensure shins are vertical ($90^\circ$) at peak extension.
*   **Pelvic Bone Bruising:** Always use a thick barbell foam pad to protect the **iliac crests and pubic bone** from direct compressive trauma.
