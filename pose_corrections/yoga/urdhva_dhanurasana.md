# 🧘‍♀️ Urdhva Dhanurasana / Chakrasana (ऊर्ध्वधनुरासन / चक्रासन - Wheel / Upward Bow Pose)

*   **Classification:** Sagittal plane spine backbend / full-body extension; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Erector Spinae](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#3-intermediate-layer-intrinsic-back-muscles)**, **[Rhomboid Major/Minor](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-layer-appendicular-back-muscles)**, **[Gluteus Maximus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region--deep-hip-rotators)**, **[Quadriceps](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-thigh)**.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Transversus Abdominis, Adductor Magnus.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Gluteus maximus inserts into the gluteal tuberosity of the femur and the IT Band; rhomboids insert into the medial border of the scapula.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Lie supine, bend knees, place feet flat on the floor close to your hips (parallel, hip-width apart).
2.  Place palms flat on the floor beside your ears, fingers pointing toward your shoulders.
3.  Press through feet and hands to lift your hips and chest, arching your body into a bridge.
4.  Actively push your chest toward the wall behind you, extending through the thoracic spine and shoulders.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **SI Joint & Lumbar Pinching (Knee Flaring):** Letting feet turn out and knees flare wide inactivates the hamstrings and over-activates gluteus maximus, compressing the **sacroiliac (SI) joint** and pinching the **L5-S1 lumbar segment**. Keep feet parallel, thighs squeezing toward midline (engaging adductors), and lift from the mid-back (Rhomboids/Erectors).
*   **Wrist Overload:** Use Hasta Bandha (distribute weight across palm perimeter and fingers) to avoid excessive carpal tunnel compression.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine squeezing a yoga block between your inner thighs to maintain neutral leg alignment.
*   *Resist:* Push the floor away through hands and feet to distribute extension along the entire spine.
