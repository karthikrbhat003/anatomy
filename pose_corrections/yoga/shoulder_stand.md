# 🧘‍♀️ Shoulder Stand (Sarvangasana)

*   **Classification:** Sagittal plane neck hyperflexion; Closed Kinetic Chain (shoulders/arms fixed).
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists (stretched):* **[Splenius Capitis](../../muscles/regions/01_head_neck_face/anatomy.md#4-lateral--posterior-cervical-muscles)**, **[Splenius Cervicis](../../muscles/regions/01_head_neck_face/anatomy.md#4-lateral--posterior-cervical-muscles)**, **[Upper Trapezius](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-layer-appendicular-back-muscles)**.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Erector Spinae, Core.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Upper trapezius originates at the superior nuchal line of the occipital bone.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Lie on your back, lift your legs and hips, and support your lower back with your hands.
2.  Walk your hands down your back toward your shoulder blades to lift your torso vertically.
3.  Ensure your body weight rests entirely on your outer shoulders and triceps, NOT your neck.
4.  Gaze straight up at your toes.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Cervical Spine Compression:** Do not try to press your entire cervical spine flat against the mat. This hyperflexes the spine and crushes the vertebrae under body weight. Leave a tiny natural arch (cervical lordosis) under your neck so that it does not touch the floor.
*   **Head Turning:** **CRITICAL WARNING:** Never turn your head side-to-side in a shoulder stand; this can cause severe cervical joint strain, ligament sprains, or disc herniation.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine your shoulders and triceps are a solid table supporting your weight, and your neck is a suspension bridge.
*   *Resist:* Actively press the elbows down to lift the hips higher.
