# 🧘‍♀️ Garudasana (गरुडासन - Eagle Pose)

*   **Classification:** Standing single-leg balance / bilateral joint binding; frontal & transverse planes; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Lower Body (Binding/Standing):* **[Gluteus Medius/Minimus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)** (standing leg balance), **[Hip Adductors (Adductor Longus/Magnus, Gracilis)](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-medial-thigh-adductors)**, **[Tensor Fasciae Latae](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)**.
    *   *Upper Body (Binding):* **[Rhomboids / Middle Trapezius](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-extrinsic-back-muscles)** (eccentrically stretched under maximal protraction), **[Serratus Anterior](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)**, **[Deltoid (Posterior)](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)**.
    *   *Stabilizers:* **[Quadriceps Femoris](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#3-anterior-thigh-quadriceps)**, **[Tibialis Posterior/Anterior](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#5-anterior-leg)**, Deep Core.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Medial femoral condyles; spine of scapula; calcaneus.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  **Chair Squat Setup:** Stand in Tadasana, bend both knees slightly into a shallow Chair Pose (Utkatasana).
2.  **Leg Wrap:** Shift weight to your left foot. Lift your right thigh high and cross it snugly over the left thigh. Hook the top of your right foot behind the left calf/Achilles tendon (or rest the right toes on a block if tight).
3.  **Squeeze Midline:** Strongly squeeze both inner thighs and adductors together along the central sagittal axis.
4.  **Arm Binding:** Extend arms forward. Cross your left arm over your right arm at the elbows, bend elbows to $90^\circ$, and wrap the forearms until palms touch.
5.  **Shoulder Alignment:** Lift your elbows to shoulder height and push your forearms away from your face to maximize **Scapular Protraction**. Sit deeper into the standing hip.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Knee Joint Torsion / Meniscal Pinches:** Forcing the foot to hook behind the calf without sufficient hip internal rotation and adduction torques the **knee joint**, stressing the **Medial Collateral Ligament (MCL)** and menisci. If the foot does not wrap naturally, keep the foot crossed without hooking the toes.
*   **Shoulder Impingement:** Avoid hiking the shoulders up into the ears during the arm bind. Maintain **Scapular Depression** to clear the subacromial space.
*   **Pelvic Rotation Cheating:** Do not let the pelvis twist toward the side of the wrapped leg; keep both **Anterior Superior Iliac Spines (ASIS)** pointing straight forward.
