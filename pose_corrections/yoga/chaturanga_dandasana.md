# 🧘‍♀️ Chaturanga Dandasana (चतुरङ्ग दण्डासन - Four-Limbed Staff Pose)

*   **Classification:** Low horizontal pushing hold; sagittal plane; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Triceps Brachii](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-arm-brachium)**, **[Pectoralis Major/Minor](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)**, **[Anterior Deltoid](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)**.
    *   *Synergists:* **[Serratus Anterior](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)**, Infraspinatus, Subscapularis.
    *   *Stabilizers:* **[Transverse Abdominis](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#3-abdominal-wall)**, **[Quadriceps Femoris](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#3-anterior-thigh-quadriceps)**, Gluteals, Deep Cervical Flexors.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Coracoid process and acromion of scapula; olecranon process of ulna.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  **Plank Setup & Shift:** Start in a high plank with wrists directly under shoulders. Shift your weight forward onto your tiptoes so your shoulders move past your wrist creases.
2.  **$90^\circ$ Elbow Descent:** Lower your body until your upper arms are parallel to the floor and your elbows form a strict **$90^\circ$ right angle**.
3.  **Elbow Adduction:** Hug your elbows tight against your lateral rib cage; do not allow them to wing outward.
4.  **Forearm Alignment:** Keep your forearms strictly vertical (perpendicular to the floor).
5.  **Straight-Body Line:** Maintain a straight axis from the crown of your head through your heels, engaging your core and quadriceps.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **The "Anterior Shoulder Dump" (Humeral Head Dropping):** Dipping the chest below elbow height causes the head of the humerus to translate anteriorly and superiorly, grinding against the **acromion process** and tearing the **anterior glenohumeral labrum**. Never let the shoulders dip below elbow level.
*   **Elbow Flaring:** Letting the elbows flare out to the sides in a $T\text{-shape}$ increases shear on the **rotator cuff tendons (supraspinatus)**. Hug elbows tightly against the lateral ribs.
*   **Lumbar Lordotic Sag:** Allowing the pelvis to collapse pinches the **$L_5\text{–}S_1$ facet joints**. Maintain active Transverse Abdominis bracing and posterior pelvic tilt.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine hovering your body like a rigid wooden staff exactly 4 inches above the floor.
*   *Resist:* Grip the mat with your finger pads (Hasta Bandha) and actively pull your collarbones forward while reaching your heels back.
