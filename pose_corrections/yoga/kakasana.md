# 🧘‍♀️ Kakasana / Bakasana (काकासन / बकासन - Crow & Crane Pose)

*   **Classification:** Isometric arm balance; sagittal/transverse plane; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Serratus Anterior](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)** (scapular protraction), **[Rectus Abdominis](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#3-anterior--lateral-abdominal-wall)** (trunk flexion).
    *   *Synergists:* Pectoralis Major, Anterior Deltoid, Triceps Brachii, Forearm Flexors.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Deep Core (TVA).
*   **Key Landmarks:** Serratus anterior attaches to the medial border of the scapula; rectus abdominis inserts into the xiphoid process and costal cartilages of ribs 5-7.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Squat down (Malasana), place hands on the floor shoulder-width apart, fingers spread wide (Hasta Bandha).
2.  Nestle your knees high up on your outer upper armpits / triceps.
3.  Shift your body weight forward, keeping your gaze slightly forward (not down at feet).
4.  Dome your upper back (serratus anterior protraction) and round your spine (rectus abdominis contraction).
5.  Draw your heels close to your glutes as your feet lift off the floor.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Wrist Joint Overloading (Flat Back Sinking):** Sinking passively into retracted shoulder blades transfers your entire body weight into the bones of the **wrist and elbow**, causing acute wrist compression. Dome the upper back (serratus protraction) to distribute weight through the shoulder girdle.
*   **Hasta Bandha (Camber Hand):** Claw the fingertips to prevent median nerve compression inside the carpal tunnel.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine you are a cat doming your back to push the ground away.
*   *Resist:* Draw the knees tight to the chest, resisting gravity through abdominal compression.
