# 🧘‍♀️ Virabhadrasana II (वीरभद्रासन २ - Warrior II)

*   **Classification:** Standing asymmetric hip opening / lunge; frontal & transverse plane; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Lead Leg:* **[Quadriceps Femoris](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#3-anterior-thigh-quadriceps)**, **[Gluteus Maximus/Medius](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)** (external rotation), **[Sartorius](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#3-anterior-thigh-quadriceps)**.
    *   *Trailing Leg:* **[Gluteus Medius/Minimus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)** (abduction & stability), **[Tensor Fasciae Latae](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)**, **[Peroneus Longus/Brevis](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#6-lateral-leg)** (arch support).
    *   *Upper Body:* **[Deltoid (Middle/Posterior)](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)**, Rhomboids, Serratus Anterior.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Medial femoral epicondyle; greater trochanter of femur; lateral malleolus of fibula.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  **Stance Alignment:** Step feet wide apart ($3.5\text{–}4\text{ feet}$). Turn front foot forward ($90^\circ$) and back foot slightly inward ($10^\circ\text{–}15^\circ$), aligning the front heel with the back arch (heel-to-arch alignment).
2.  **Lead Knee Tracking:** Bend the front knee into a strict $90^\circ$ angle, stacking the knee directly above the ankle. Track the knee outward toward the second and third toes.
3.  **Back Leg Drive:** Ground down firmly through the outer edge of the back foot, engaging the back quad to lock the knee in extension.
4.  **Pelvis & Torso Neutrality:** Keep the torso upright and centered between both hips (do not lean forward over the front thigh).
5.  **Arm Extension:** Reach arms out parallel to the floor, actively depressing the scapulae, and gaze softly over the front middle finger (Drishti).

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Front Knee Valgus Collapse:** Allowing the lead knee to drift inward inside the big toe places high tensile shear on the **Medial Collateral Ligament (MCL)** and wears down the **medial meniscus**. Actively fire the **Gluteus Medius and External Rotators** of the front hip to track the knee over the second toe.
*   **Back Foot Arch Collapse:** Rolling onto the inner edge of the back foot strains the **anterior talofibular ligament** and medial arch. Press firmly through the **outer heel and lateral border** of the rear foot.
*   **Anterior Pelvic Tilt & Lumbar Over-Arching:** Tucking the tailbone slightly (neutral pelvis) prevents facet jamming in the lumbar spine.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine trying to tear your yoga mat in half between your feet by pulling your front heel forward and pushing your back foot backward.
*   *Resist:* Wrap your front outer hip back and under to maintain a true $90^\circ$ thigh angle.
