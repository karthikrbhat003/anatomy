# 🧘‍♀️ Ustrasana (उष्ट्रासन - Camel Pose)

*   **Classification:** Sagittal plane neck/spine backbend; Closed Kinetic Chain (knees fixed).
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[SCM](../../muscles/regions/01_head_neck_face/anatomy.md#4-lateral--posterior-cervical-muscles)** and **[Longus Colli](../../muscles/regions/01_head_neck_face/anatomy.md#4-lateral--posterior-cervical-muscles)** (contracting eccentrically to control head weight).
    *   *Stabilizers:* Erector Spinae, Gluteus Maximus, Core.
*   **Key Landmarks:** SCM inserts into the mastoid process of the temporal bone; the hyoid bone serves as a stabilizer anchor for neck muscles.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Kneel on the floor, hips stacked directly over your knees.
2.  Place your hands on your lower back (fingers pointing down) and lift your chest.
3.  Arch your spine backward, reaching for your heels if accessible.
4.  Instead of dropping your head back completely, keep the front of your throat active and draw your ears away from your shoulders.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Passive Neck Collapse:** Throwing the head back completely with zero muscle engagement compresses the **vertebral arteries** (which can cause dizziness) and hyper-compresses the **C5-C7 spinal segments**. Keep SCM and deep flexors engaged to support the head in a safe, active arc.
*   **Lumbar Compression:** Squeeze your glutes and push your hips forward to keep them stacked over the knees, protecting the lower back.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine lifting the base of your skull away from your shoulders as you arch back.
*   *Resist:* Actively press your shins down to drive the hips forward.
