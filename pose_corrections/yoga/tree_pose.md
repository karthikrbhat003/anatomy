# 🧘‍♀️ Tree Pose (Vrksasana)

*   **Classification:** Single-leg balance hold; frontal plane; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* Standing Leg: **[Gluteus Medius](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region--deep-hip-rotators)**, **[Gluteus Minimus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region--deep-hip-rotators)**. Lifted Leg: **[Piriformis](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region--deep-hip-rotators)**, **[Obturator Internus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region--deep-hip-rotators)**.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Core, Foot arch stabilizers.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Gluteus medius and deep rotators insert into the greater trochanter of the femur.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Stand tall on your left leg. Lift your right foot and place the sole on your inner left calf or thigh (avoiding the knee joint).
2.  Press the sole of the right foot and standing leg together.
3.  Draw your right knee out to the side (external rotation).
4.  Contract the standing left gluteus medius, pulling the outer hip inward. Keep hip points level.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Standing Hip Flare (IT Band Strain):** Letting your standing hip flare out to the side (and pelvic drop on the other side) stretches and inflames the **iliopectineal bursa** and strains the **IT Band** and **gluteal tendons**. Actively contract the standing gluteus medius to pull the outer hip inward, leveling the hip points.
*   **Knee Pressure:** Never place the foot directly on the knee joint; this pushes the knee sideways, straining the lateral ligaments.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine your standing leg is a tree trunk growing roots deep into the earth.
*   *Touch:* Squeeze your standing hip to feel the outer glute muscle contract and pull the hip bone level.
