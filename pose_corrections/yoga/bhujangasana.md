# 🧘‍♀️ Bhujangasana (भुजङ्गासन - Cobra Pose)

*   **Classification:** Sagittal plane spinal backbend; Open/Closed hybrid.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Erector Spinae](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#3-intermediate-layer-intrinsic-back-muscles)**, **[Multifidus](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#4-deep-layer-transversospinalis-muscles)**.
    *   *Synergists:* Rhomboids, Latissimus Dorsi, Gluteus Maximus.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Transversus Abdominis.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Erector spinae run along the spinal column; the spine of the scapula serves as the insertion for upper/lower trapezius.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Lie prone on the mat, legs extended, tops of feet pressing firmly into the floor.
2.  Place palms flat beside your chest, elbows tucked close to your ribs.
3.  Using only your back muscles, lift your chest off the floor. Keep hands light.
4.  Roll shoulders down and back away from your ears.
5.  Gaze slightly forward and down, keeping the cervical spine continuous with the thoracic curve.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Lumbar Hinging (Facet Joint Compression):** Do not push yourself up high using arm force if your spinal extensors are inactive. This collapses the curve into the **L5-S1 lumbar segment**, pinching spinal facet joints. Lift from the thoracic spine.
*   **Cervical Compression:** Do not crank your head backward. This pinches suboccipital nerves and compresses the **cervical facet joints (C1-C4)**. Keep the back of the neck long.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine sliding your sternum forward through your upper arms rather than just arching straight up.
*   *Resist:* Draw the shoulders down to actively resist upper trapezius shrugging.
