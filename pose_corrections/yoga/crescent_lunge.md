# 🧘‍♀️ Crescent Lunge (Anjaneyasana)

*   **Classification:** Sagittal plane asymmetrical hold; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* Front Leg: **[Gluteus Maximus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region--deep-hip-rotators)**, **[Quadriceps](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-thigh)**. Back Leg: **[Iliopsoas](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#4-posterior-abdominal-wall)** (stretched).
    *   *Synergists:* Adductor Magnus, Sartorius.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Gluteus Medius, Transversus Abdominis.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Hip flexors attach at the lesser trochanter of the femur and the lumbar vertebrae bodies.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  From a standing position, step one foot back into a deep lunge. Lift your back heel.
2.  Bend your front knee until it is directly over your ankle at $90^\circ$.
3.  Tuck your tailbone slightly down, pulling your front hip points up (posterior pelvic tilt).
4.  Extend arms overhead, keeping shoulders relaxed.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Lumbar Arching & SI Joint Shear:** Allowing the hip flexor tension of the back leg to pull your pelvis forward into an extreme arch (anterior pelvic tilt) compresses the **lower back facet joints** and shears the **sacroiliac (SI) joint**. Bend your back knee slightly to level the pelvis, engaging the lower core (TVA).
*   **Knee Tracking:** Keep the front knee aligned directly over the ankle. Do not let it overshoot the toes or cave inward.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine pulling your front heel and back toes toward each other to activate the pelvic floor.
*   *Resist:* Squeeze your back glute (antagonist) to tell the back hip flexors to release (reciprocal inhibition).
