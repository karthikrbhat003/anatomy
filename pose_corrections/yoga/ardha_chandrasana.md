# 🧘‍♀️ Ardha Chandrasana (अर्धचन्द्रासन - Half Moon Pose)

*   **Classification:** Standing single-leg balance / lateral hip abduction; frontal plane; Closed Kinetic Chain (standing leg) / Open Kinetic Chain (floating leg).
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Standing Leg:* **[Gluteus Medius/Minimus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)** (eccentric/isometric pelvic stabilization), **[Quadriceps Femoris](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#3-anterior-thigh-quadriceps)**, **[Tibialis Posterior](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#7-posterior-leg-deep)**.
    *   *Floating Leg:* **[Gluteus Medius](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)** (abduction), **[Tensor Fasciae Latae](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)**, **[Gluteus Maximus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)**.
    *   *Torso & Core:* **[Internal/External Obliques](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#3-abdominal-wall)**, **[Quadratus Lumborum](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#2-deep-intrinsic-back-muscles)**, Erector Spinae.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Greater trochanter of standing femur; anterior superior iliac spine (ASIS).

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  **Transition & Setup:** From Warrior II or Triangle Pose, place your front hand on a yoga block or the floor $6\text{–}12\text{ inches}$ ahead and slightly to the pinky-toe side of your standing foot.
2.  **Standing Leg Rooting:** Straighten the standing leg with a micro-bend at the knee, grounding through the tripod of the foot.
3.  **Hip Stacking:** Lift the back leg parallel to the floor ($90^\circ$ hip abduction). Externally rotate and stack the top hip directly over the bottom hip.
4.  **Foot Flexion:** Strongly flex the top foot (**Dorsiflexion**), pushing through the heel and pointing the toes sideways.
5.  **Chest & Arm Opening:** Rotate your chest open to the side wall, stacking the top shoulder over the bottom shoulder, and extend the top arm straight up toward the ceiling.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Standing Knee Hyperextension (Genu Recurvatum):** Locking the standing knee backward into hyperextension transfers shear force onto the **Posterior Cruciate Ligament (PCL)** and posterior knee capsule. Keep an active micro-bend engaged with the quadriceps.
*   **Trendelenburg Pelvic Drop:** If the standing **Gluteus Medius** is weak, the pelvis tilts downward toward the floor, pinching the standing femoral head into the acetabulum. Actively fire the standing outer glute to maintain horizontal pelvic elevation.
*   **Top Hip Internal Rotation:** Letting the top hip turn downward toward the floor converts the pose into a twisted forward fold, torquing the standing **Sacroiliac (SI) Joint**. Keep the top ASIS stacked over the bottom ASIS.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine your entire body is flattened between two narrow panes of glass.
*   *Resist:* Push through your top heel into an imaginary wall behind you to activate your lateral fascial chain.
