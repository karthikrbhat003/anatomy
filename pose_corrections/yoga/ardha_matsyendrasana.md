# 🧘‍♀️ Ardha Matsyendrasana (अर्धमत्स्येन्द्रासन - Half Lord of the Fishes / Seated Spinal Twist)

*   **Classification:** Seated asymmetrical spinal rotation / visceral compression; transverse plane; Closed/Open Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Rotating Muscles (Agonists):* **[Internal Oblique (Ipsilateral)](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#3-abdominal-wall)**, **[External Oblique (Contralateral)](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#3-abdominal-wall)**, **[Multifidus](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#2-deep-intrinsic-back-muscles)**, **[Rotatores](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#2-deep-intrinsic-back-muscles)**.
    *   *Elongated (Stretched):* **[Piriformis](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)**, **[Gluteus Medius/Maximus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region)**, Rhomboids.
    *   *Stabilizers:* **[Erector Spinae](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#2-deep-intrinsic-back-muscles)** (axial elongation), Deep Cervical Rotators.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Thoracic vertebral facet joints ($T_1\text{–}T_{12}$); lumbar facet joints ($L_1\text{–}L_5$ oriented in sagittal plane permitting only $\approx 5^\circ$ rotation); greater trochanter.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  **Seated Setup:** Sit with both sits bones grounded on the mat. Bend your left knee and place your left heel outside your right hip. Cross your right foot over your left thigh, placing the right sole flat on the floor outside the left knee.
2.  **Axial Decompression:** Inhale deeply and lengthen the spine upward toward the ceiling, creating maximum intervertebral disc spacing before initiating rotation.
3.  **Thoracic Rotation:** Exhale and rotate your torso toward the right, hugging the right knee with the left arm or hooking the left elbow outside the right knee.
4.  **Sequential Spiral:** Initiate the twist from the navel (lower ribs), spiral through the **Thoracic Spine ($T_1\text{–}T_{12}$)**, and finish with the neck and gaze over the right shoulder.
5.  **Back Hand Anchor:** Place your right fingertips lightly on the floor behind your sacrum like a kickstand to keep the spine tall without leaning backward.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Lumbar Facet Impingement (Forced Lumbar Twisting):** The sagittal orientation of lumbar facet joints permits only $\approx 5^\circ$ of total axial rotation. Forcing rotation through the lower back causes facet jamming and annular disc tearing. **$85\%$ of spinal rotation must occur through the thoracic spine ($T_1\text{–}T_{12}$)**.
*   **Slumping into Spinal Kyphosis:** Twisting with a rounded spine places massive uneven shear loads on the intervertebral discs. Never rotate without prior **axial spinal elongation**.
*   **Levering with the Arm:** Forcibly reefing on the knee with the elbow to wrench the torso deeper bypasses core rotators and overstretches the **Sacroiliac (SI) Joint ligaments**. Use active oblique muscle contraction to drive the rotation.

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)

*   *Resist:* Ground both sits bones evenly into the mat as anchors, wringing out the abdomen on every exhalation like a sponge.
