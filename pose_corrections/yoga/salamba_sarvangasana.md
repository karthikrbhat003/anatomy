# 🧘‍♀️ Salamba Sarvangasana (सालम्ब सर्वाङ्गासन - Supported Shoulder Stand)

*   **Classification:** Inversion / sagittal plane cervical flexion; Closed Kinetic Chain (shoulders/arms fixed).
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists (stretched):* **[Splenius Capitis](../../muscles/regions/01_head_neck_face/anatomy.md#4-lateral--posterior-cervical-muscles)**, **[Splenius Cervicis](../../muscles/regions/01_head_neck_face/anatomy.md#4-lateral--posterior-cervical-muscles)**, **[Upper Trapezius](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-layer-appendicular-back-muscles)**.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Erector Spinae, Triceps Brachii, Core, Gluteus Maximus.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Upper trapezius originates at the superior nuchal line of the occipital bone.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Lie supine, lift your legs and hips, and support your mid-back with your hands.
2.  Walk your hands closer toward your shoulder blades to lift your torso vertically.
3.  Ensure your body weight rests entirely on your outer shoulders and triceps, NOT your cervical spine.
4.  Gaze straight up at your toes.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Cervical Spine Compression:** Do not press your entire cervical spine flat against the mat. This hyperflexes the spine and crushes vertebrae under body weight. Leave a small natural arch (cervical lordosis) under your neck.
*   **Absolute Prohibition on Turning the Head:** **CRITICAL WARNING:** Never turn your head side-to-side in Sarvangasana; rotational shear under axial load can cause acute cervical facet dislocation or disc herniation.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine your shoulders and triceps are a solid foundation supporting your body, while your neck remains free of compression.
*   *Resist:* Actively press your triceps and elbows down to elevate the spine.
