# 🧘‍♀️ Cobra Pose (Bhujangasana)

*   **Classification:** Sagittal plane spinal backbend; Open/Closed hybrid.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Erector Spinae](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#3-intermediate-layer-intrinsic-back-muscles)**, **[Multifidus](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#4-deep-layer-transversospinalis-muscles)**.
    *   *Synergists:* Rhomboids, Latissimus Dorsi, Gluteus Maximus.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Transversus Abdominis.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Erector spinae run along the spinal column; the spine of the scapula serves as the insertion for upper/lower trapezius.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Lie face down, legs extended, tops of feet pressing into the mat.
2.  Place hands flat on the floor next to your chest, elbows tucked close to your ribs.
3.  Using only your back muscles, lift your chest off the floor. Keep hands light.
4.  Roll shoulders down and back, away from your ears.
5.  Gaze slightly forward and down, keeping the back of the neck long.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Lumbar Hinging (facet joint compression):** Do not push yourself up high using your arms if your back muscles aren't active. This collapses the curve into the **L5-S1 lumbar segment**, pinching the spinal facet joints. Lift from the thoracic spine.
*   **Tech-Neck Crunch:** Do not crank your head up to look at the ceiling. This pinches the suboccipital nerves and compresses the **cervical spine facet joints (C1-C4)**. Keep the back of the neck long and wrinkles-free.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine sliding your chest forward through your upper arms rather than just lifting up.
*   *Resist:* Draw the shoulders down to actively resist the upper trapezius.
