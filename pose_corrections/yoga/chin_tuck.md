# 🧘‍♀️ The Chin Tuck (Yoga / Postural)

*   **Classification:** Sagittal plane neck retraction; Closed/Open Kinetic Chain hybrid.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Longus Colli](../../muscles/regions/01_head_neck_face/anatomy.md#4-lateral--posterior-cervical-muscles)**, **[Longus Capitis](../../muscles/regions/01_head_neck_face/anatomy.md#4-lateral--posterior-cervical-muscles)** (deep neck flexors).
    *   *Antagonists (stretched):* Suboccipital muscles (rectus capitis posterior, obliquus capitis).
*   **Key Landmarks:** Longus colli attaches to the anterior arch of the atlas (C1) and bodies/transverse processes of C3-T3.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Sit or stand upright with your shoulders relaxed and gaze pointing straight ahead.
2.  Without looking down at the floor, slide your head straight backward (like a drawer closing) to make a "double chin."
3.  Hold for 2–3 seconds, feeling a gentle stretch at the base of the skull.
4.  Release back to neutral and repeat for 10–12 repetitions.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **SCM Dominance (Nodding Down):** Avoid nodding your head down or looking at your chest. This engages the superficial **SCM** rather than the deep neck flexors, which actually worsens forward head posture (tech-neck). Keep your chin parallel to the floor the entire time.
*   **Jaw Clenching:** Do not clench your teeth; keep your jaw relaxed to prevent **TMJ** joint strain.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine a string pulling the crown of your head straight up to the ceiling while you pull your ears back.
*   *Touch:* Place a finger on your throat to feel the deep muscles activate as your head slides back.
