# 🧘‍♀️ Bridge Pose (Setu Bandhasana)

*   **Classification:** Sagittal plane spine backbend / hip extension; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Gluteus Maximus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region--deep-hip-rotators)**, **[Levator Ani](../../muscles/regions/05_pelvis_perineum/anatomy.md#3-deep-compartment-urogenital-diaphragm)** (pelvic floor / root lock).
    *   *Synergists:* Hamstrings, Erector Spinae.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Transversus Abdominis, Adductor Magnus.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Levator ani originates from the pubis inner surface and ischial spine; inserts into the perineal body and coccyx.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Lie flat on your back, bend knees, place feet flat on the floor close to your hips (parallel, hip-width apart).
2.  Press through your feet to lift your hips. Place a block between your thighs and squeeze it.
3.  As you lift, engage **Mula Bandha** (draw the pelvic floor up).
4.  Clasp your hands under your back, rolling your shoulders under to lift the chest.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **SI Joint & Sacrum Pinching:** Squeezing the glutes as hard as possible without inner thigh engagement causes the knees to flare wide, compressing the **sacrum and SI joints**. Squeezing a block between your thighs activates the inner thigh adductors, which directly co-activates the pelvic floor to level and stabilize the pubic symphysis and SI joint.
*   **Cervical Loading:** Keep your weight on your shoulders and feet; do not let it load the neck.

---

*   *Touch:* Squeeze the block between your thighs to feel the adductors fire, instantly relaxing lower back pinching.
