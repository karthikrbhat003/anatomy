# 🧘‍♀️ Vrikshasana (वृक्षासन - Tree Pose)

*   **Classification:** Single-leg balance hold; frontal plane; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* Standing Leg: **[Gluteus Medius](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region--deep-hip-rotators)**, **[Gluteus Minimus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region--deep-hip-rotators)**. Lifted Leg: **[Piriformis](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region--deep-hip-rotators)**, **[Obturator Internus](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region--deep-hip-rotators)**.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Deep Core, Foot intrinsic stabilizers.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Gluteus medius and deep rotators insert into the greater trochanter of the femur.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Stand tall on your left leg (Tadasana). Lift your right foot and place the sole on your inner left calf or inner thigh (strictly avoiding the knee joint).
2.  Press the sole of the right foot and standing inner thigh firmly against one another.
3.  Draw your right knee outward to the side (external hip rotation).
4.  Contract the standing left gluteus medius, drawing the outer hip inward. Keep both hip points level and facing forward.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Standing Hip Flare (IT Band Strain):** Letting your standing hip flare outward (and pelvis drop on the opposite side) strains the **IT Band** and inflames the **trochanteric bursa**. Actively contract the standing gluteus medius to pull the outer hip inward, keeping the pelvis level.
*   **Knee Joint Pressure:** Never place the foot directly against the medial aspect of the knee joint; lateral pressure compromises the knee collateral ligaments.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine your standing leg is a rooted tree trunk anchoring deep into the earth.
*   *Touch:* Touch your standing outer hip to verify the gluteus medius is contracted firmly.
