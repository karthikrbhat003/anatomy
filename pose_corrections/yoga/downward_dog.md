# 🧘‍♀️ Downward-Facing Dog (Adho Mukha Svanasana)

*   **Classification:** Multi-planar, inversion hold; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Serratus Anterior](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)** (protraction), **[Deltoid (Anterior)](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)** (flexion).
    *   *Synergists:* Triceps Brachii, Infraspinatus, Teres Minor.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Quadriceps, Tibialis Anterior, Core.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Serratus anterior attaches to the medial border of the scapula, pulling it flat against the thoracic cage.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Start on hands and knees, hands slightly forward of shoulders, fingers spread wide.
2.  Tuck your toes, lift your knees, and send your hips back and up.
3.  Push down firmly through your index finger and thumb knuckles.
4.  Roll your outer armpits toward the floor (externally rotating the shoulders).
5.  Reach your heels toward the mat, keeping a micro-bend in the knees if your hamstrings are tight.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Acromion Pinch:** Do not let your shoulders collapse and shrug up into your ears. This retracts and elevates the scapula, narrowing the subacromial space and pinching the **supraspinatus tendon**. Actively rotate your shoulders outward ("wrap your armpits forward").
*   **Hamstring Pull:** Squeezing the quadriceps triggers reciprocal inhibition, telling the hamstrings to relax, protecting their origin at the **ischial tuberosity** from micro-tears.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine trying to "screw" your palms into the floor (right hand clockwise, left hand counter-clockwise) to lock in the shoulder stabilizers.
*   *Resist:* Resist the floor by pushing it away to lengthen your spine.
