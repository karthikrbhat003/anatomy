# 🧘‍♀️ Jalandhara Bandha / Griva Sanchalana (जालंधर बन्ध / ग्रीवा सञ्चालन - Chin Tuck & Cervical Alignment)

*   **Classification:** Sagittal plane neck retraction / cervical lock; Closed/Open Kinetic Chain hybrid.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Longus Colli](../../muscles/regions/01_head_neck_face/anatomy.md#4-lateral--posterior-cervical-muscles)**, **[Longus Capitis](../../muscles/regions/01_head_neck_face/anatomy.md#4-lateral--posterior-cervical-muscles)** (deep neck flexors).
    *   *Antagonists (stretched):* Suboccipital muscles (rectus capitis posterior, obliquus capitis).
*   **Key Landmarks:** Longus colli attaches to the anterior arch of the atlas (C1) and bodies/transverse processes of C3-T3.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Sit in a comfortable meditative posture (Padmasana, Sukhasana, or Vajrasana) with your spine upright and gaze forward.
2.  Without looking down at the floor, slide your head straight backward (like a drawer closing) to engage the deep cervical flexors.
3.  For Jalandhara Bandha, gently draw the chin down toward the jugular notch of the sternum while lifting the chest up to meet it, maintaining length in the back of the neck.
4.  Hold for 2–3 breath cycles, feeling a gentle decompression at the base of the skull.
5.  Release slowly back to neutral.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **SCM Dominance (Nodding Down):** Avoid aggressively jerking the head down or compressing the throat with the superficial **SCM**. Keep the back of the neck long to engage the deep stabilizer flexors.
*   **Jaw Clenching:** Keep your teeth slightly parted and jaw relaxed to prevent **TMJ** joint strain.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine a string pulling the crown of your head straight up to the ceiling while you pull your ears back.
*   *Touch:* Place a finger on your throat to feel the deep stabilizer muscles activate as your head slides back into alignment.
