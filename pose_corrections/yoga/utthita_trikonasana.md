# 🧘‍♀️ Extended Triangle Pose (Utthita Trikonasana)

*   **Classification:** Frontal plane asymmetrical hold / lateral flexion & hip opener; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists (strengthening):* Standing Leg: **[Gluteus Medius](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#1-gluteal-region--deep-hip-rotators)** (lateral hip stability), **[Quadriceps](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-thigh)** (to stabilize knees). Torso: **[Obliques (Internal/External)](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#3-anterior--lateral-abdominal-wall)** (lateral spine stabilizers).
    *   *Antagonists (stretching):* Front Leg: **[Hamstrings](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-thigh)** (especially the medial head: Semitendinosus, Semimembranosus), **[Adductors](../../muscles/regions/06_lower_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-thigh)**.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Hamstrings originate at the **ischial tuberosity**; Gluteus Medius inserts into the **greater trochanter** of the femur.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Stand with feet wide (about 3.5 to 4 feet apart). Turn your front toes $90^\circ$ outward, back foot turned slightly inward ($15^\circ$).
2.  Inhale and extend your arms parallel to the floor, shoulder height.
3.  Exhale, reach your torso forward over the front leg, shifting your hips back toward the back heel.
4.  Hinge from your front hip joint (not the waist) and fold laterally, placing your bottom hand on your shin, a block, or the floor.
5.  Extend your top arm straight up toward the ceiling, spinning your chest open. Keep both sides of your waist long.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Knee Hyperextension (Front Leg):** Locking the front knee out completely and pushing it backward under lateral load forces the **posterior knee capsule** into hyperextension, risking joint injury and ligament strain. Keep a micro-bend in the front knee, actively pulling the kneecap upward (engaging the quadriceps).
*   **Lumbar Side Collapsing:** Sinking or rounding the lower side of the waist toward the floor places a heavy lateral shearing force on the **lumbar vertebrae**. Engage both sides of your obliques to pull the lower ribs away from the hips, keeping the spine straight and long.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine your entire body is pressed flat between two narrow panes of glass.
*   *Touch:* Place a hand on the underside of your waist; ensure the muscles are firm and the ribs are expanded forward, not collapsed.
