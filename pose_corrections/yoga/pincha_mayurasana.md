# 🧘‍♀️ Pincha Mayurasana (पिञ्च मयूरासन - Feathered Peacock / Forearm Stand)

*   **Classification:** Forearm inversion / overhead shoulder flexion; sagittal plane; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Serratus Anterior](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)** (upward rotation & elevation), **[Deltoid (Anterior/Lateral)](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)**, **[Triceps Brachii](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#2-muscles-of-the-arm-brachium)**.
    *   *Synergists:* **[Trapezius (Upper/Lower)](../../muscles/regions/02_back_spine/anatomy.md#1-superficial-extrinsic-back-muscles)**, **[Rotator Cuff (Infraspinatus/Teres Minor)](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)**, Forearm Pronators/Flexors.
    *   *Stabilizers:* **[Transverse Abdominis](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#3-abdominal-wall)**, **[Rectus Abdominis](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#3-abdominal-wall)**, Gluteus Maximus, Adductors.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Olecranon process of ulna; acromion process of scapula; costal ribs.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  **Forearm Foundation:** Place forearms on the mat strictly shoulder-width apart, parallel to each other like the number 11 (use a yoga strap above elbows or a block between hands if elbows tend to splay).
2.  **Dolphin Pose Entry:** Tuck toes, lift hips into Dolphin Pose, and walk your feet as close to your elbows as possible, elevating your hips over your shoulders.
3.  **Active Shoulder Elevation:** Press down forcefully through your forearms, wrists, and palms, pushing the floor away to elevate the shoulder girdle and create maximum space between shoulders and ears.
4.  **Controlled Kick-Up:** Lift one leg to the ceiling, take a light hop with the bottom leg, and bring both legs together vertically.
5.  **Hollow Body Stack:** Squeeze inner thighs, point toes, tuck ribs in, and engage the core into a **Posterior Pelvic Tilt** to maintain a straight vertical column.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Elbow Splaying & Shoulder Impingement:** Allowing the elbows to slide wider than shoulder-width forces the shoulders into internal rotation and adduction under heavy compressive load, pinching the **supraspinatus tendon** against the **acromion**. Keep forearms parallel.
*   **The "Banana Back" Lumbar Collapse:** When shoulder active flexion is limited ($<180^\circ$), athletes compensate by arching excessively through the lower back, causing severe compressive shear on the **$L_4\text{–}L_5\text{ and }L_5\text{–}S_1$ facet joints**. Engage the lower abdominals and knit the front ribs in.
*   **Wrist Creeping Inward:** Prevent the hands from sliding together into a triangle; keep palms flat and fingers gripping the mat.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine pressing the floor away so aggressively that your torso becomes completely weightless.
*   *Resist:* Squeeze an imaginary yoga block between your inner elbows to keep the shoulder stabilizers locked.
