# 🧘‍♀️ Adho Mukha Svanasana (अधोमुखश्वानासन - Downward-Facing Dog)

*   **Classification:** Multi-planar, inversion hold; Closed Kinetic Chain.
*   **Muscles Involved:**
    *   *Agonists:* **[Serratus Anterior](../../muscles/regions/03_thorax_abdomen/anatomy.md#1-anterior-thorax--pectoral-region)** (protraction & upward rotation), **[Deltoid (Anterior)](../../muscles/regions/04_upper_limb/anatomy.md#1-shoulder--rotator-cuff-muscles)** (flexion).
    *   *Synergists:* Triceps Brachii, Infraspinatus, Teres Minor.
    *   *Stabilizers:* Quadriceps, Tibialis Anterior, Core.
*   **Key Landmarks:** Serratus anterior attaches to the medial border of the scapula, pulling it flat against the thoracic cage.

---

## 🏋️ Proper Execution
1.  Start on hands and knees, hands slightly forward of shoulders, fingers spread wide (Hasta Bandha).
2.  Tuck your toes, lift your knees, and send your sits bones back and upward.
3.  Push down firmly through the base of the index finger and thumb knuckles.
4.  Roll your outer armpits toward the floor (externally rotating the humerus).
5.  Reach your heels toward the mat, keeping a micro-bend in the knees if hamstrings are tight.

---

## 🛡️ Joint Safety & Precautions
*   **Acromion Pinch & Impingement:** Do not let your shoulders collapse and shrug into your ears. This retracts and elevates the scapulae, narrowing the subacromial space and pinching the **supraspinatus tendon**. Actively rotate shoulders outward ("wrap outer armpits forward").
*   **Hamstring Protection:** Squeezing the quadriceps triggers reciprocal inhibition, allowing hamstrings to lengthen safely and protecting their origin at the **ischial tuberosity**.

---

## 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection (Sensory Cues)
*   *Visualize:* Imagine trying to "screw" your palms into the floor (right hand clockwise, left hand counter-clockwise) to lock in the shoulder stabilizers.
*   *Resist:* Push the floor away to lengthen the spine from wrists to pelvis.
