# Rule: Documentation Formatting & Visual Clarity

## 🚫 1. No Mermaid Graphs
*   **Rule:** Do NOT use `mermaid` diagrams (`graph TD`, `graph LR`, `flowchart`, `sequenceDiagram`, etc.).
*   **Requirement:** Always format classifications, divisions, and workflows into clean, high-contrast **Markdown Tables**, **Comparison Matrices**, or **Structured Callout Boxes**.

## 🎨 2. Visual Clarity & Anti-Clutter Standards for Generated Images
*   **Zero Text Overlap:** Diagrams must be spacious, clean, and minimalist with large, crisp, legible typography. Strictly avoid dense, crowded multi-panel collages with tiny overlapping or distorted text.
*   **Maximum 2–3 Panels:** Restrict infographics to at most 2 or 3 well-spaced, focused panels.
*   **High-Contrast Color Coding:** Use bold, unmistakable labels and distinct directional vectors (e.g., cyan/orange).
