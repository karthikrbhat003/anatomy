# 🧘‍♂️ Applied Anatomy & Biomechanics for Movement Arts

Welcome to your personal repository for learning anatomy step-by-step! This repository contains a structured, deep, day-by-day curriculum focused on **biomechanics and movement science**—specifically tailored for **calisthenics progression** and **yoga posture correction**—before moving into internal organs and systems.

---

## 🎯 Project Core Objectives
*   **Biomechanics First:** Master joint structures, force distributions, muscle activation, and kinematic chains.
*   **Calisthenics Progression:** Apply anatomical knowledge to high-level skills (e.g. handstands, pushing, and pulling).
*   **Yoga Posture Correction:** Understand alignments (e.g. pelvic tilt, spinal extension) to optimize postures and prevent injury.
*   **Systemic Transition:** Progress into cardiovascular, respiratory, digestive, and endocrine systems in depth.

---

## 📚 Curriculum Structure (At a Glance)

Our learning path is split into 6 core modules:

1.  **🟢 Module 1: Foundations of Movement Science & Biomechanics (Days 1–7)**
    *   Planes, axes, muscle contractions, fascia, and open/closed kinetic chains.
2.  **🔵 Module 2: Shoulder Girdle & Upper Body Biomechanics (Days 8–14)**
    *   Scapular movements, the rotator cuff, shoulder impingement, and pushing/pulling mechanics.
3.  **🟡 Module 3: Spine, Pelvis & Core Biomechanics (Days 15–21)**
    *   Vertebral anatomy, pelvic tilts, core stabilization, spinal bends, and postural assessment.
4.  **🟠 Module 4: Hip Girdle & Lower Body Biomechanics (Days 22–28)**
    *   Hip joint mechanics, hamstrings, knee tracking, ankle dorsiflexion, and squat/balance depth.
5.  **🔴 Module 5: Neuromuscular Mechanics & Injury Prevention (Days 29–35)**
    *   Proprioception (GTO/spindles), stretch reflex (PNF), fascial lines, and warm-up design.
6.  **🟣 Module 6: Systemic Anatomy & Internal Organs (Days 36–40+)**
    *   Cardiovascular, respiratory, digestive, lymphatic, and endocrine-nervous system integrations.

## 🚀 How to Start Your Learning
1.  Open the central progress tracker: [`tracker.md`](./tracker.md).
2.  Navigate to the current day's notes folder.
3.  Study the **Core Anatomical Concepts** and complete the **Movement Practice Drill**.
4.  Watch the curated **Reference Videos**.
5.  Test yourself with the **Daily Knowledge Check** (revealed in the details tab).
6.  Mark the topic as `Completed` in [`tracker.md`](./tracker.md) and commit your progress!
